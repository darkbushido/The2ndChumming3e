# Megacorporations
{: .no_toc }

The giants in 2060–61: who owns each one, where it's based, what it does in
Seattle, and what running against it is like.

## How they got here  *(SR3 p.31–35)*

- **2033: Damien Knight's "Nanosecond Buyout".** A burst of computer-driven
  trades gave Knight 22% of **Ares**. He and CEO Leonard Aurelius loathed
  each other from the start *(SR3 p.31)*.
- **2034: Richard Villiers** bought a failed tech company cheaply after its
  founders died, then used its "lost" research to buy his way into
  **Fuchi** *(SR3 p.31)*.
- **2037: Lofwyr** revealed that he owned 63% of the company behind BMW, and
  renamed it **Saeder-Krupp** *(SR3 p.31)*.
- **2041–42: Yamatetsu** forced its way onto the Corporate Court, turning
  the Big Seven into the **Big Eight** *(SR3 p.31)*.
- **2048: Operation Reciprocity.** The corps struck Aztechnology jointly
  after Aztlan nationalised them *(SR3 p.31)*.
- **2058–60: The corp war.** Fuchi broke apart into Novatech, Renraku and
  Shiawase. **Yamatetsu** moved its headquarters to Vladivostok rather than
  depose its new ork chairman. **Wuxing** and **Cross** took Corporate Court
  seats *(SR3 p.34–35)*.

### Ares Macrotechnology  *(NS p.89–90)*

The military-industrial giant, led mostly by ex-military execs. In Seattle
it avoids a costly war with Federated-Boeing and works with it instead, as a
sub-contractor and **ally**. Its security subsidiary **Knight Errant**
guards its buildings, and Ares Arms tests prototypes on KE. Its feud with
**Cross** is personal: Leonard Aurelius, Knight's old rival, sold his Ares
stock and backed Cross, and his son now runs Cross's Seattle division
*(NS p.91)*.

### Aztechnology  *(NS p.81, p.90)*

Banned from California and Tir Tairngire, so Seattle is where it pulls the
strings on its business there, through shells and holding companies. At
least one battalion of **Aztlan troops** is stationed in Seattle. Runners
describe a layered magical defence of watcher spirits and then elementals,
and rumours of **blood magic** in the sub-basements. Its Matrix host has a
link to the Tenochtitlán home office. It is **Aztlan's corp** in all but
name, and relations between Aztlan and Seattle are "cool but cordial"
*(NS p.81)*.

### Cross Applied Technologies  *(SR3 p.35; NS p.90–91)*

A second-tier corp from Quebec pushing into the UCAS. It survived the Big
Eight's attempts to keep it out and took Renraku's old Corporate Court
seat. Its black-ops division is **the Seraphim**. Runners say Cross prefers
to take intruders alive, and its Matrix ice traces you so it can collect a
favour later. Its people have been seen visiting the Draco Foundation.

### The Draco Foundation
{: #the-draco-foundation }

Set up by Dunkelzahn's will to administer his bequests, with Nadja Daviar
as chair *(SR3 p.34)*. In Seattle it is run by **Sassinian**, a sasquatch.
The Council of Princes refused it an office in the Tir, so the Seattle
office covers the Tir as well. *New Seattle* calls it "a big-big-big
employer of shadowrunners": it has plenty of money and a will to carry out
*(NS p.91–92)*. It also sits on the UCC *(NS p.83)*.

### Mitsuhama Computer Technologies  *(NS p.92–93)*

It aims for the top spot in computing now that Fuchi and Renraku are hurt.
Runners credit it with the **"zero-zone"**: zero penetration, zero
survival. That means concealed autoguns, lethal gas, barghest guard dogs,
bound elementals, and **black ice** that kills. Its wage mages are
**Unit 13**, which is mostly human, with no shamans. Its Johnsons want the
job done exactly as planned. It lost its yakuza backing when the Seattle
clans seceded.

### Novatech  *(SR3 p.35; NS p.93–94)*

Richard Villiers's third of Fuchi, merged into his own business empire.
His ex-wife **Samantha Villiers** runs Seattle *(NS p.93)*. Villiers offered
Miles Lanier the job of head of security *(SR3 p.35)*. Novatech is hungry to rebuild Fuchi's reputation, and it hires
runners freely for that. William Ager, Fuchi's former "resources
adjuster", now runs black ops for Novatech in Seattle. Runners warn that he
is racist. Novatech is still fighting **Renraku** over the old Fuchi files.

### Renraku Computer Systems  *(SR3 p.35; NS p.94)*

Badly hurt. In December 2059 its Seattle arcology went off-line, and the
UCAS military moved in *(SR3 p.35)*. It survives on its alliance with the
Nakatomi family's third of Fuchi. Its acting director, **Tadashi
Marushige**, is described as a soldier fighting a war. Runners say its
Seattle security is "double-ultra-paranoid", with cascading black ice.

### Saeder-Krupp  *(SR3 p.31; NS p.94–95)*

Owned by the great dragon **Lofwyr**, and called the world's largest
megacorp. Officially it has no presence in Seattle. Unofficially it has
subsidiaries, small offices, and watchers posted by Lofwyr. "The biggest
business Saeder-Krupp does in Seattle is shadow business," and nobody can
predict what it will want.

### Shiawase  *(NS p.76, p.95–96)*

The Shiawase decision that created extraterritoriality is named after this
corp *(SR3 p.22)*. Shiawase Atomics maintains Seattle's power grid, and
wants Gaeatronics's generating contract *(NS p.76)*. It is moving into
cyberware with former Fuchi assets. Its spy network often knows a run is
coming. Its defences use **net-guns and knockout gas**, and its ice is
mostly trace, because it wants prisoners to interrogate.

### Wuxing  *(SR3 p.35; NS p.81, p.96)*

A small family corp until Dunkelzahn's money raised it to the top. Wuxing
replaced Fuchi on the Corporate Court. It drives the **Pacific Prosperity
Group**, a trade bloc against Japanese economic dominance. That makes it an
enemy of the Japanese corps, and a rival of Federated-Boeing, which is also
in the PPG. Runners expect the **Triads** to work on its behalf.

### Yamatetsu  *(SR3 p.35; NS p.96)*

When Yuri Shibanokuji, an ork, inherited the chairmanship in 2059,
Yamatetsu moved its headquarters to **Vladivostok** rather than depose
him. It now sells itself as the meta-friendly, "Awakened" corp, and its
traditionalist staff are fighting that change. It has no friends among the
other Japanese corps, and runners expect it to court Wuxing and Novatech.

## Seattle's own big players  *(NS p.83–89)*

Seattle's local corps employ more of the city than the megacorps do, and
they have the home-field advantage *(NS p.83)*.

| Corp | What it is | Ties |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Federated-Boeing** | Seattle's largest employer: aircraft, drones, ships | Ares and Mitsuhama have both failed to buy it. Member of the PPG *(NS p.84, p.96)*. |
| **Brackhaven Investments** | Kenneth Brackhaven's holding company | Funds politicians across every party. His uncle chairs Humanis *(NS p.83–84)*. |
| **Gaeatronics** | Supplies most of Seattle's power | Owned by the Salish-Shidhe Council. Runners say it backs eco-terrorists against competitors *(NS p.84–85)*. |
| **Pacific Rim Communications** | Runs Seattle's LTG phone network | Buying up telecomm contracts Fuchi lost *(NS p.87)*. |
| **Telestrian Industries** | Tir Tairngire's largest conglomerate | 5% owned by the Council of Princes, runners claim *(NS p.87–88)*. |
| **Universal Omnitech** | Biotech | Owns Ingersoll & Berkley. Rival of Telestrian *(NS p.85, p.88)*. |
| **United Oil** | Petrochemicals, Dallas (CAS) | Its security chief was once a dragon, Haesslich *(NS p.88)*. |
| **Microdeck, Dassurn, Global Technologies, MegaMedia, VisionCrafters, VisionQuest** | Computers, finance, skillsofts, media, optics, simsense | All of them hire runners for paydata and against each other *(NS p.84–89)*. |
