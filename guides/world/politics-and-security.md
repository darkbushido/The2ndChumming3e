# Politics, Police & Policlubs
{: .no_toc }

Who enforces the law, who lobbies and bombs for a cause, and how Seattle
deals with its neighbours, as of October 2059. These are the powers that
aren't megacorps or syndicates, but deal with both.

## Law enforcement is a business
{: #law-enforcement-is-a-business }

Seattle fired its whole police department in **2025**, after the officers
went on strike, and gave policing to **Lone Star** *(SR3 p.28; NS p.76)*.
Police work is now "a business like any other" *(NS p.76)*.

| Provider | Owner and base | Role in Seattle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lone Star Security Services** | Austin, Texas | **The** police, under city contract. It has authority over every other security corp in the metroplex *(NS p.76, p.78)*. |
| **Knight Errant** | Ares (Damien Knight's own project), Detroit | Second-largest provider. It holds private contracts and has a training academy in Renton. Its image is elite, while Lone Star's is "beat cops" *(NS p.77)*. |
| **Eagle Security, Hard Corps, Wolverine** and others | Various | At least twenty firms in all. They may act only on their client's property, and must hold a suspect there until Lone Star arrives *(NS p.78)*. |

- **The contract is always contested.** Knight Errant wants Lone Star's
  contract, and so does the Sioux-based Eagle Security, though the city is
  unlikely to trust a NAN corp *(NS p.76)*.
- **The police hire runners too.** Lone Star Seattle's **Shadowriders** are
  a covert unit that hires freelance runners for not-quite-legal jobs against
  the syndicates, and has people ready to take the blame if a job goes wrong
  *(NS p.76)*.
- **The "Dips"**, Lone Star's magical investigation division (DPI), read
  crime scenes astrally and **license Seattle's legal magicians**. That
  gives them leverage over above-ground magic *(NS p.77)*.

### The Metroplex Guard  *(NS p.78)*

Seattle's own military is the **Metroplex Guard**. It has quietly kept
metahumans out of its ranks, and it's an open secret that **Humanis**
recruits from it: some Guard officers and senior NCOs hold posts in Humanis.

## Policlubs
{: #policlubs }

*New Seattle* sorts policlubs into three camps: anti-Awakened and
anti-metahuman, pro-metahuman, and pro-civil rights. It also sorts them by
type: **political and fraternal organisations**, **terrorist groups**, and
a rumoured third kind, secretive circles of the wealthy *(NS p.79)*.

| Policlub | Leader in Seattle | Stance | Backing and links |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Humanis Policlub** | Karl Brackhaven | Human supremacist. Treats metahumanity as a disease. | Founded **2023** *(SR3 p.28)*. Publicly it only lobbies. Privately it **fronts for and funds** terrorists such as **Alamos 20,000** and the **Hand of Five**, and steers its most violent members to them *(NS p.79–80)*. |
| **Ork Rights Commission (ORC)** | Jasper Squashright | Metahuman rights, focused on orks | Grassroots, but runners say it has a real war chest and pays runners well. Runners also claim its leaders pass resources to pro-Awakened terrorists such as the **Sons of Sauron** *(NS p.80)*. |
| **Empowerment Coalition** | Libby Chalmers | Civil rights | Funded by Dunkelzahn's **Timmons Memorial Fund**. It is bringing ORC and other groups under its umbrella, and will pay runners *(NS p.80)*. |

**How they interact:** Humanis has rich friends. **Kenneth Brackhaven**, an
investor and Karl Brackhaven's nephew, sits on the UCC and has paid for the
campaigns of at least six district mayors *(NS p.83–84)*. The Coalition's
Libby Chalmers reportedly faced him down when he told her the Coalition
wasn't wanted in Seattle *(NS p.80)*. Each side's terrorists are its
deniable hand, much as shadowrunners are the corps'.

{: .note }
> **The Human Nation** appears in two ways. NS p.79 names it in the rumoured
> third category, a circle of the very rich funding experiments on
> metahumans. NS p.80 then lists it among Humanis's terrorist fronts.
> The book leaves the contradiction unresolved.

## Foreign relations
{: #foreign-relations }

Seattle is a UCAS city surrounded by NAN territory. Since 2042 it has been
allowed to send its own envoys abroad *(NS p.80)*. Its position goes back
to the Treaty of Denver, which kept cities like Seattle as extraterritorial
extensions of their governments *(SR3 p.27)*.

| Neighbour | Relations  *(NS p.81)* |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Aztlan** | Cool but cordial. Seattle is Aztechnology's main West Coast market, which gives Aztlan leverage. Aztlan once tried to dock a warship in Everett. |
| **California Free State** | Mixed. CalFree sees Seattle as an ally against the Tir, and its spies use Seattle as a base for operations there. |
| **Japanese Imperial State** | Losing ground to the Pacific Prosperity Group. |
| **Korea** | Growing ties through the PPG and Wuxing. |
| **NAN / Salish-Shidhe** | Tense. Some in the NAN want Seattle for themselves. |
| **Tir Tairngire** | Strained since Dunkelzahn's death, but the Tir needs Seattle as a trade pipeline and a place to send its dissidents. |

### How the nations got here  *(SR3 p.27–30)*

The Treaty of Denver gave the **Native American Nations** most of western
North America *(SR3 p.27)*. The **CAS** broke from the newly merged
UCAS in 2034. The elves founded **Tír na nÓg** (2034) and **Tir Tairngire**
(2035), and the dragon Lofwyr sits on Tir Tairngire's Council of Princes.
California's 2036–37 collapse left **San Francisco** under Japanese
corporate control *(SR3 p.30)*.
