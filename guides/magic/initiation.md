# How to Initiate
{: .no_toc }

How an Awakened character climbs the grades of initiation: what it costs,
the ordeals that make it cheaper, magical groups, metamagic, and the geasa
that keep a magician's power when Magic is lost. Initiation isn't in the
core rulebook; all of this is from *Magic in the Shadows* *(MitS p.30–33,
p.57–79)*.

1. TOC
{:toc}

---

## Who can initiate  *(MitS p.57–58)*

Only the Awakened: **full magicians, aspected magicians and adepts**. A
character can initiate **alone** or as a member of a **magical group**,
which makes it cheaper. Each initiation adds a **grade**: the first makes
you a Grade 1 initiate, the second Grade 2, and so on.

The gamemaster decides when a character can initiate, and some time should
pass between having the Karma and taking the next grade. They also decide
what the process involves, guided by the character's path: study, practice,
rituals, or proving themselves *(MitS p.57–58)*.

## What each grade gives  *(MitS p.58)*

**Every initiate** gets these with each grade:

- **Astral Reaction** goes up by the grade.
- An **Astral Pool** of dice equal to the grade. It can be used on any test
  on the astral plane or the metaplanes, including Astral Combat (even with
  Sorcery) and Astral Perception, except Magical Skill Tests. It refreshes
  like other pools.
- A full magician's **first** initiation opens the **metaplanes** of astral
  space (p.91).

**Then choose one** of these three, each time:

| Option | Magic | And |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Learn a metamagical technique** | +1 | One technique you qualify for. [Below](#metamagic). |
| **Alter your astral signature** | +1 | A Magic Test against **6 + the new grade**, with no dice but Karma. Two or more successes change it. Each failure adds +1 to later attempts. |
| **Shed a geas** | none | One geas comes off a Magic point you already have, so that point works without it. [Geasa](#geasa). |

- **Magic can rise above 6** this way.
- **Adepts get a Power Point** for each point of Magic gained. See
  [Physical Adepts](../physical-adepts/).

> **The book's example** *(MitS p.58)*: Bandi, Magic 8, initiates to Grade
> 3 and tries to alter her signature. She rolls 8 dice against 6 + 3 = **TN
> 9** and gets 1 success, which isn't enough. Next time her TN is one higher.

## The Karma cost  *(MitS p.58)*

**Base cost = 5 + the grade you're initiating to.** Multiply by the
initiation type, and round down.

| Initiation Cost Table *(MitS p.58)* | Cost |
| :--- | :--- |
| Self-initiation | Base Cost × 3 |
| Self-initiation with an ordeal | Base Cost × 2.5 |
| Group initiation | Base Cost × 2 |
| Group initiation with an ordeal | Base Cost × 1.5 |

The same table worked out for the first six grades:

| Grade | Base | Self | Self + ordeal | Group | Group + ordeal |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 1 | 6 | 18 | 15 | 12 | 9 |
| 2 | 7 | 21 | 17 | 14 | 10 |
| 3 | 8 | 24 | 20 | 16 | 12 |
| 4 | 9 | 27 | 22 | 18 | 13 |
| 5 | 10 | 30 | 25 | 20 | 15 |
| 6 | 11 | 33 | 27 | 22 | 16 |

> **The book's example** *(MitS p.58)*: Eli, an owl shaman, initiates
> alone. Grade 1 costs (5 + 1) × 3 = **18**, or **15** with an ordeal. Grade
> 2 costs 7 × 3 = **21**, or 7 × 2.5 = 17.5, rounded down to **17**, with an
> ordeal. In a group he'd have paid 6 × 2 = **12** for Grade 1, or 6 × 1.5 =
> **9** with an ordeal, and **14**, or **10** with an ordeal, for Grade 2.

## Ordeals  *(MitS p.58–61)*

An ordeal is a test of the candidate. It lowers the cost multiplier by
**0.5**. Choose it **before** initiating; only a deed can be done ahead of
time. Pass it and you gain the grade. Fail and you try again, without paying
the Karma a second time. An ordeal can be repeated only with the GM's
permission, and an oath never.

| Ordeal | What it takes |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Astral quest** | A quest on the metaplanes with a rating of **twice the grade**. Shamans go to their totem's metaplane, mages to each of the four elemental metaplanes. You must be able to project. After a failure, a Willpower Test against the grade sets the preparation time: 10 days divided by the successes. |
| **Asceticism** | Permanently give up **1 point of a Physical Attribute**'s unaugmented rating, and 1 point of its racial modified limit. Not an Attribute at 1. Taken again, it must be a different Attribute first. |
| **Deed** | A task fitting the character's path, group, totem or code, agreed with the GM, or a run that fits. **You give up that run's Karma award.** It can be saved, but only for the next initiation. |
| **Familiar** | Magicians who can summon allies only. Summon an **ally spirit** that costs at least the initiation's Karma (with the ordeal). It costs no Magic Point, but if it's ever banished, freed or destroyed you check for Magic loss. |
| **Geas** | Take a geas on the new grade's Magic Point; an adept can put it on the new Power Point. It can never be removed. |
| **Meditation** | Pass three tests: Charisma against your Strength, Intelligence against your Quickness, Willpower against your Body. Each takes grade × 4 days, divided by its successes. Fail one and the cycle starts again. +2 to all three on a Squatter or Street lifestyle, unless you're in the wilderness with Wilderness Survival 4+. |
| **Oath** | Swear to obey a magical group's rules. For group members only, and only the first oath counts. |
| **Thesis** | Write or make a formula of all your magical knowledge, as a spell formula of rating 6, 30 days base time. Anyone with a copy has a material link to you, and if every copy is destroyed you lose a Magic Point. |

## Magical groups  *(MitS p.61–65)*

Orders, covens, lodges and circles. What makes one magical is a **magical
link**, its connection to a spirit **avatar**. Initiating through a group
is cheaper, but members live by its rules.

- **Kinds** *(MitS p.62)*: **initiatory** (magical study first),
  **dedicated** (a religious or moral cause) and **conspiratorial** (power
  and wealth, often secret and controlling).
- **Joining** costs **3 Karma and the first month's dues**, which other
  members can pay for you. The group then checks that your "vibes" fit its
  link *(MitS p.65)*.
- **Founding** one needs two or more Awakened, each paying the 3 Karma.
  Once a month they roll to establish a link, against TN 12 with modifiers;
  extra dice cost 3 Karma each *(MitS p.65)*.
- **Leaving** a group you swore an oath to means a check for **Magic loss**
  *(MitS p.62)*.

### Strictures  *(MitS p.62–63)*

A group's rules, tied to its magic. Most have three or four that bind each
member:

> Attendance · Belief · Deed · Exclusive Membership · Exclusive Ritual ·
> Fraternity · Geas · Obedience · Sacrifice (−1 to every Karma award) ·
> Secrecy

**Group strictures**, such as **Limited Membership** or **Oath** (every
member takes the oath ordeal at their first initiation with the group), can
only be broken by the group itself, and doing so costs it its link.

**Breaking a stricture** leaves marks in your aura that fellow members can
see. When you next initiate, roll **Magic** against **twice the grade
you're seeking**, needing at least one success per stricture broken. Pass
and the slate is wiped clean. Fail and the group must expel you, or lose
its link *(MitS p.62)*.

## Metamagic  *(MitS p.69–79)*
{: #metamagic }

| Technique | Page | What it does |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Anchoring** | p.70 | Store a live spell in an anchoring focus, set off by a trigger. |
| **Centering** | p.72 | Use a creative skill to add successes, resist Drain, or cancel penalties. [Below](#centering). |
| **Cleansing** | p.74 | Clear temporary background count, once its cause is gone. |
| **Divining** | p.74 | Read omens for insight into future events. |
| **Invoking** | p.75 | Summon great form spirits. |
| **Masking** | p.76 | Disguise your aura, and see through others' disguises. |
| **Possessing** | p.76 | Your astral form enters and controls a living being. |
| **Quickening** | p.77 | Make a sustained spell permanent without a sustaining focus. |
| **Reflecting** | p.78 | Spell defense that can turn a spell back on its caster. |
| **Shielding** | p.79 | A stronger form of spell defense. |

### Learning a technique  *(MitS p.69–70)*

One technique per grade, and only one you can use: conjurers and adepts
can't learn Quickening or Shielding, which need Sorcery. **Adepts can learn
only Centering, Divining and Masking.** There are three ways to learn:

| From | The test | Time and price |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Another initiate** of equal or higher grade who knows it | Magic, TN **8 − the teacher's grade** | 14 days ÷ successes. Free within a group; otherwise usually **grade × 1,000¥ a day**. |
| **An astral quest** | The quest, rated by the number of techniques you already know (TN at least 2) | Days equal to the quest rating. |
| **A free spirit** | As from an initiate, with Spirit Energy for grade | Karma, at least its Spirit Energy. Invoking costs double. |

Fail the test and you can try that technique again only at your next grade,
on top of that grade's technique. A third failure, or a roll of **all 1s**,
means you can never learn it.

### Centering  *(MitS p.72–74)*
{: #centering }

Centering uses two skills: **Centering**, an Active Magical Skill linked to
Willpower, and a **creative skill** such as singing, dancing, an instrument,
an arcane language or meditation. Centering can never be higher than the
creative skill, and the creative skill can't come from a chip. Centering is
a **Free Action** in the same phase as the magic it helps, rolled first, and
you get only one of its benefits per phase:

- **For success**: same TN as the magical test. Every 2 successes add a
  success, but only if the magical test scores at least one. A magician has
  to be astrally perceiving to do this.
- **Against Drain**: TN = the Drain Power. Every 2 successes count as one
  Drain Resistance success, if the Drain test scores at least one.
- **Against penalties**: TN = the magical test's full TN **minus your
  grade**. Every 2 successes cancel 1 point of modifiers, never the base TN.

**Adept centering** *(MitS p.73–74)*. An adept who learns Centering applies
it to **Athletics and Stealth**. Each later grade can add one more skill
area instead of another technique: Build and Repair, Melee Combat, Ranged
Combat, Knowledge, Language, Social, Technical, Vehicle, or a Special Skill
area. Adepts don't need astral perception to center for success, and can
center against the Drain of their powers, such as Attribute Boost.

> **The book's example** *(MitS p.73)*: Beaumains, a Grade 4 initiate,
> casts Powerbolt at a target with Body 4, partial cover (+2), poor light
> (+2), and his own Light Stun (+1): TN 9. He centers on his synthesizer
> against 9 − 4 = **TN 5** and gets 2 successes, cancelling 1 point. He
> casts at **TN 8**.

## Geasa  *(MitS p.30–33)*
{: #geasa }

When a character **loses a point of Magic**, they can take a **geas**, a
restriction on how they use magic, to keep working as if they still had
it. A magician with Magic 6 who loses a point is at 5, but with a geas casts
as if they had 6.

- Decide **when the point is lost**. Turn the geas down then and you can't
  take one for that point later.
- At most **unaugmented Willpower** geasa. At Magic 0 nothing helps: the
  character is mundane.
- A geas only offsets lost points; it never adds Magic.
- **Break any one** and you lose **all** the points your geasa cover, and
  take **+1 to every Magical Skill test per geas** you have, while it stays
  broken. Astral combat and assensing aren't affected.
- Initiation can shed one geas per grade, instead of gaining Magic.

**Standard geasa** *(MitS p.32)*: Condition (unwounded, astrally
perceiving…), Domain (the city, the forest…), Exclusive (adepts only: the
power can't be used alongside other magic), Fasting, Gesture, Incantation,
Shaman's (only spells and spirits with a totem advantage), Talisman, and
Time (day or night, or a season).

> **The book's example** *(MitS p.33)*: Rikki Ratboy, Magic 6, loses a
> point to a bad wound and takes the **gesture** geas, then another to
> cyberware and takes **domain** (the city). Shot in a park with a broken
> arm, he's broken both. He works at Magic **4**, with **+2** to all magical
> tests, until he's out of the park and his arm is fixed.

## Doing it in The 2nd Chumming (Foundry)
{: #doing-it-in-the-2nd-chumming-foundry }

The system records the grade but doesn't run initiation yet.

- **Initiate Grade** is a field on the **Magic** tab. A ward's **🌫 Fool This
  Ward** test reads it (Masking, against a ward).
- **The Karma**: work out the cost from the table above, and subtract it
  from Good Karma on the sheet. The Spend Karma calculator doesn't offer
  initiation yet.
- **Magic**: raise the Magic Attribute by hand. An adept's Power Point total
  follows Magic, so their new Power Point appears by itself.
- **Not tracked yet**: the initiate's Astral Pool and Astral Reaction bonus,
  metamagic, geasa and magical groups. Apply them at the table.
