# Physical Adepts
{: .no_toc }

The Awakened who turn their magic inward, on their own bodies and minds:
Power Points, every adept power in the core rulebook, the ones *Magic in
the Shadows* adds, and how the sheet tracks them. From the core rulebook
*(SR3 p.54–55, p.160, p.168–171)* and *(MitS p.149–151)*.

1. TOC
{:toc}

---

## What an adept is  *(SR3 p.55, p.168)*

Adepts follow **the somatic way**. They don't cast spells or conjure
spirits, and they have no access to astral space unless they buy the
**Astral Perception** power. They can never astrally project. What they get
instead are **powers**, magical abilities that become a natural part of the
adept through training *(SR3 p.55, p.168)*. An adept can still use Sorcery
in astral combat *(SR3 p.168)*.

- **At character creation** an adept takes **Magic at Priority B**, the
  same priority as an aspected magician; a full magician needs Priority A
  *(SR3 p.54)*.
- Adepts get **no Spell Points**. They can buy weapon foci with nuyen, but
  can't bond one until they have the Karma *(SR3 p.55, p.60)*.

## Power Points  *(SR3 p.55, p.168)*

- An adept starts with **Power Points equal to their Magic Attribute**, 6
  in most cases. At character creation they **can't buy or sell** extra
  Power Points *(SR3 p.55)*.
- Each power costs Power Points. **Once spent they're gone, and the choice
  of powers is permanent** *(SR3 p.168)*.
- **No power can have more levels than the adept's Magic.** An adept with
  Magic 4 has at most 4 points of powers, and none of more than four levels
  *(SR3 p.168)*.
- **During play**, an adept can buy more Power Points at **20 Good Karma
  each** *(SR3 p.168)*. See [Karma](../../rules/karma/).

### Losing Magic  *(SR3 p.160, p.168)*

Every full point of Essence lost, or fraction of one, costs a point of
Magic: an adept with Essence 5.8 and Magic 5 loses another point of Magic
when Essence drops below 5. Deadly wounds, bad medical care, drug abuse and
disruption in astral combat can also call for a **Magic Loss check**: roll
2D6, and if the total is **less than or equal to** your Magic, it drops by
one. A roll of 2 always costs the point *(SR3 p.160)*.

**An adept who loses a point of Magic loses a point's worth of powers.**
The player chooses which *(SR3 p.168)*. At Magic 0 the character burns out
and is mundane forever *(SR3 p.160)*.

### Attribute Boost  *(SR3 p.168–169)*
{: #attribute-boost }

1. **Activate it** with a **Magic Test** against **half the base
   (unaugmented) Attribute**, rounded up. No successes, no boost.
2. Otherwise the Attribute goes up by the power's level, for **Combat Turns
   equal to the successes**. Nothing can be boosted past **twice its racial
   modified limit** (p.245).
3. **When it runs out**, resist Drain with **Willpower** against **half the
   boosted Attribute**, rounded up. Every two successes reduce the Drain
   Level by one. The damage is **Stun**.

| Attribute Boost Drain Table: the boosted rating is… | Drain Level |
| :--- | :---: |
| At or below the racial modified limit | L |
| Up to the racial Attribute maximum | M |
| Up to twice the racial modified limit | S |

It doesn't work with cyberware or spell increases, but does stack with
Improved Physical Attribute.

**Worked example.** A human adept with base Strength 5 and Attribute Boost
(Strength) 2 activates it. Magic Test TN: 5 ÷ 2 = 2.5, rounded up, **3**.
She rolls 3 successes, so Strength is **7** for **3 Combat Turns**. A human's
Strength limit is 6 and maximum 9 *(SR3 p.245)*; 7 is above 6 but no higher
than 9, so the Drain is **M**. When the boost ends she rolls Willpower
against 7 ÷ 2 = 3.5, rounded up, **4**. Two successes would drop the Drain to
Light; four would cancel it.

### Improved Ability  *(SR3 p.169)*
{: #improved-ability }

- The dice apply to the skill and carry over equally to its specializations.
- **Capped at the base skill rating or Magic, whichever is less.** An adept
  with Pistols 4 and Magic 5 can have at most 4 dice.
- **Defaulting** to the improved skill uses only **half** the dice, rounded
  down.
- It doesn't raise the skill's rating, only the dice rolled for it.

### Killing Hands  *(SR3 p.170)*
{: #killing-hands }

Ordinary unarmed attacks do **(Strength)M Stun**. With Killing Hands the
adept may instead do **Physical** damage at the level bought, and must
**declare it with the attack**. It works against Immunity to Normal Weapons
(their defensive bonuses don't count), and in astral combat if the adept has
Astral Perception and is using it.

### Missile Parry  *(SR3 p.110, p.170)*
{: #missile-parry }

A Free Action. Roll **Reaction**, plus any Combat Pool dice you want to
add, against **10 minus the base target number for the attack's range**.
You catch the missile only with **more successes than the attacker**; ties
go to the attacker.

{: .note }
> The base range TNs are Short 4, Medium 5, Long 6 and Extreme 9
> *(SR3 p.110)*, so a long-range arrow is TN 10 − 6 = **4**. The book's own
> example says "10 − 8, the base Target Number for long range", giving 2.
> The rule sentence, not the example, is followed here and in the system.

### Pain Resistance  *(SR3 p.170)*
{: #pain-resistance }

Subtract the level from your damage **before** looking up the injury
modifier. It doesn't reduce the damage itself. The book's example: with
Pain Resistance 3, Light and Moderate wounds carry no modifier, and 4 boxes
of damage count as 4 − 3 = 1, a Light wound, **+1**. It works on both
Condition Monitors, and the level also comes off target numbers to resist
pain from torture, illness and the like.

## Worked example: building an adept

A street samurai-style adept with **Magic 6** has **6 Power Points**:

| Power | Arithmetic | Power Points |
| :--- | :--- | ---: |
| Improved Reflexes 2 | from the table | 3 |
| Killing Hands (Moderate) | from the table | 1 |
| Improved Ability (Unarmed Combat) 2 | 2 dice × .5 | 1 |
| Mystic Armor 2 | 2 levels × .5 | 1 |
| | **Total** | **6** |

Her Unarmed Combat is 4, so the cap on Improved Ability is the lesser of 4
and Magic 6: **4**, and 2 dice is within it. No power has more than 6
levels. After a few runs she spends **20 Good Karma** on a seventh Power
Point and buys **Pain Resistance 2** (2 × .5 = 1).

## Doing it in The 2nd Chumming (Foundry)
{: #doing-it-in-the-2nd-chumming-foundry }

### Making a character an adept

On the **Magic** tab, set the magic type to **Adept**. The tab then shows an
**Adept Powers** list with **Power Points: used / total**, where the total
is Magic plus any Power Points bought with Karma.

Add powers by dragging them from the compendiums (*Shadowrun 3rd*, *Magic in
the Shadows* and *State of the Art 2064* under Adept Powers), or with
**+ Add Power**. Give a levelled power its level on the row.

The system **warns, and never refuses**:

- Power Points over the total turn the count red.
- A power with more levels than Magic is listed under **Above Magic**
  *(SR3 p.168)*.
- Improved Reflexes stacked with wired reflexes or a spell gets a warning,
  and the sheet applies the better package only *(SR3 p.169)*.
- Powers the system can describe but not resolve are listed as **Applied by
  the GM**, with what each one does.

### What the system does with each power

| Power | Where it shows up |
| :--- | :--- |
| Improved Physical Attribute | Added to the Attribute (per level). |
| Improved Reflexes | Reaction and Initiative dice. |
| Improved Ability | Extra dice on the skill, capped at the lesser of the skill and Magic. Half, rounded down, when defaulting. |
| Combat Sense | Extra Combat Pool. |
| Pain Resistance | A smaller wound modifier. The boxes on the Condition Monitor don't change. |
| Mystic Armor | Added to Impact armor on the soak card, and to astral soak. |
| Killing Hands | Offered in the **Called Shot** dialog of an unarmed attack, because it must be declared with the attack. It replaces the damage level; it doesn't stage it. |
| Attribute Boost | A **⚡** button on the power's row. It rolls the Magic Test, counts the turns down each Combat Turn, and posts the Drain card when the boost runs out. |
| Missile Parry | A third choice when declaring a defense against an arrow, a crossbow bolt or a thrown weapon. |
| Quick Strike | A **⚡** on the adept's row in the combat tracker, once per Combat Turn. |
| Body Control, Counterstrike, Sixth Sense, Iron Will, Rooting and the other situational dice | Added where the roll knows the situation, offered as a checkbox where it doesn't. |

### Buying a Power Point with Karma

On the **Bio** tab, **Spend Karma…** lists **Power Point** under Magic at 20
Good Karma *(SR3 p.168)*. The purchase goes into the 📒 Ledger, and the GM can
undo it there. See [Karma](../../rules/karma/#doing-it-in-the-2nd-chumming-foundry).
