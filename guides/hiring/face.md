## What the books give you

| | | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Investigation** (legwork) | **200¥/day** | SRComp p.100 |
| **Distraction** | **1,000¥** | SRComp p.100 |
| **Fencing** | the face's Negotiation moves the price **5% per net success** | SR3 p.238 |
| **Buying gear** | also **5% per net success** | SR3 p.273 |

A face earns their fee at the **Negotiation** table. Each net success is
worth 5% on a fence deal or a black-market purchase *(SR3 p.238, p.273)*,
and Mr. Johnson always opens low and leaves room to bargain *(MJLBB
p.20–21)*.

## House rates

{: .house }
> These rates aren't from any SR3 book. They are the original gist's
> numbers, kept for convenience.

### Base day rate, by Negotiation or other social skill

| Tier | Skill | Base day rate | Typical kit |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Hustler | 1–3 | 300–600¥ | Street contacts and simple bluffs |
| Professional | 4–5 | 1,500–3,000¥ | Tailored pheromones, a Rating 4 fake ID, mid-level contacts |
| Veteran | 6–7 | 4,500–9,000¥ | Strong pheromones or Kinesics, high-tier corporate contacts |
| Elite | 8+ | 15,000¥ and up | A global network and flawless impersonation |

### Threat multiplier

| Work | Multiplier |
| :--- | :---: |
| Legwork, contract negotiation, fence deals | ×1 |
| Impersonation inside a corporate facility | ×2–3 |
| Undercover in a yakuza, triad or mafia operation, or a black site | ×4–5 |

### Hazard pay and expenses

- Off-site negotiation: no hazard pay.
- **On-site**, talking the team past checkpoints: **+50% to +100%**.
- The employer pays for venues, clothing, gifts and bribes.
- **Burned identity deposit**: enough to replace the fake ID at its rating
  (see the table above).

## Worked examples

| Job | House rate | Companion baseline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Negotiating a Johnson contract, professional (base 2,000¥), ×1 | **2,000¥**, or 5–10% of the bonus they win | Investigation: 200¥ a day |
| Posing as staff in a corporate office, veteran (base 6,000¥), ×2, plus a Rating 4 fake ID | 12,000 + 16,000 = **28,000¥** | Distraction: 1,000¥; investigation: 200¥ a day |
| Undercover in a yakuza operation, veteran (base 8,000¥), ×4 | 8,000 × 4 = **32,000¥** plus bribes and burned IDs | Investigation: 200¥ a day |
