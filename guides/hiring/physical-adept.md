> - The power is **Improved Reflexes** (levels 1–3, +1D6 to +3D6 Initiative),
>   not "Increased Reflexes". Its bonus **can't be combined** with wired
>   reflexes or other technological or magical boosts. *(SR3 p.169)*
> - Initiative dice aren't Initiative Passes. The number of passes comes from
>   the Initiative score, which drops by 10 each pass. *(SR3 p.104)*
> - There are no adept powers called "Invisibility" or "Silence". For stealth,
>   look at **Traceless Walk** *(MitS p.151)* and **Melanin Control**
>   *(SOTA64 p.67–68)*.
> - "Wall-walking" is **Wall Running**. "Bushido / Way powers" isn't an SR3
>   term, so it's been removed.
> - The Scenario C total was wrong: 18,000 × 4, then +50%, is **108,000¥**,
>   not 81,000¥.

### Powers an employer might pay for

| Power | What it does | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Improved Reflexes 1–3 | +1D6 to +3D6 Initiative; doesn't stack with cyberware | SR3 p.169 |
| Killing Hands | Unarmed attacks do Physical damage; works on the astral | SR3 p.170, p.175 |
| Mystic Armor | Impact armor that also counts in astral combat | SR3 p.170, p.175 |
| Astral Perception | Lets the adept see and fight on the astral | SR3 p.169 |
| Traceless Walk, Missile Mastery | Stealth; catching and throwing missiles | MitS p.150–151 |
| Wall Running, Melanin Control | Mobility; camouflage | SOTA64 p.67–68 |
| Kinesics | Control of body language: easier social tests, harder to read | SOTA64 p.66 |

- A **weapon focus** or **Killing Hands** lets an adept hurt spirits and
  projecting magicians. That's why "spirit hunter" work pays well.
  *(SR3 p.174–175)*
- Adepts can take **Magic Loss** from Deadly wounds, just as magicians do.
  *(SR3 p.129)*

## House rates

{: .house }
> These rates aren't from any SR3 book. They are the original gist's
> numbers, kept for convenience.

### Base day rate, by combat skill and initiation

| Tier | Skill and grade | Base day rate | Typical powers |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Street athlete | Skill 1–3 | 400–800¥ | Improved Reflexes 1, a movement power |
| Professional | Skill 4–5 | 2,000–4,000¥ | Improved Reflexes 1–2, Killing Hands, a Force 2–3 weapon focus, Astral Perception |
| Veteran | Skill 6–7, grade 1–3 | 5,000–10,000¥ | Improved Reflexes 2–3, Mystic Armor, a Force 4+ weapon focus, Missile Mastery |
| Master | Skill 8+, grade 4+ | 18,000¥ and up | Spirit-killing melee and near-undetectable movement |

### Threat multiplier

| Work | Multiplier |
| :--- | :---: |
| Bodyguarding, stealth recon, static security | ×1 |
| Assaulting a perimeter, close-quarters fights with corporate guards | ×2 |
| Security mages, dual-natured critters, strong spirits | ×3–5 |

### Hazard pay

- Silent infiltration or overwatch: no hazard pay.
- **Point man or breacher**: **+50% to +100%**.

## Worked examples

| Job | House rate | Companion baseline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Nightclub bodyguard, professional (base 2,500¥), ×1 | **2,500¥** a night | **200¥** a day |
| Hunting a spirit, veteran with a weapon focus (base 7,000¥), ×3 | 7,000 × 3 = **21,000¥** | Assassination: 5,000¥ |
| Infiltration and point on a corporate assault, master (base 18,000¥), ×4, +50% | 18,000 × 4 × 1.5 = **108,000¥** | A share of the extraction or destruction fee |
