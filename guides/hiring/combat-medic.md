# Hiring a Combat Medic
{: .no_toc }

A runner with **Biotech** and a medkit, who comes along to keep the team
alive until it reaches a real doctor. No SR3 book has a "combat medic"
archetype or a price for hiring one. This page puts together what the books
**do** price, and adds house rates in the same style as the other
specialist pages.

## What the books give you

| | | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Paramedic first aid**, per wound treated | Light **50¥**, Moderate **100¥**, Serious **200¥**, Deadly **400¥** | SR3 p.128 |
| **Doctor's services**, per day | the same scale: **50¥–400¥ a day** by wound | SR3 p.128 |
| **Bodyguard/security duty**, the nearest run type | **200¥/day** | SRComp p.100 |
| **DocWagon contract**, the alternative to hiring a medic | Basic **5,000¥**, Gold **25,000¥**, Platinum **50,000¥**, Super-Platinum **100,000¥** a year | SR3 p.304 |

The book's closest character is the **DocWagon paramedic** contact: Biotech
5 with a First Aid specialisation at 7, Boosted Reflexes 1, an armor jacket
and a Rating 5 medkit. They get their thrills on the job, they're curious
about runners, and if you stay on their good side they'll patch you up
cheaply *(MJLBB p.65)*. That's a fair picture of a professional-tier medic
below.

### What a medic actually does at the table

- **First aid** reduces a Light, Moderate or Serious wound by **one level**,
  and must be given **within an hour** of the injury. On a Deadly wound it
  can only **stabilize** the patient. See the full procedure on
  [Healing](../../rules/healing/#step-2-first-aid) *(SR3 p.129)*.
- **Know when your patient is Awakened.** First aid on an Awakened patient
  carries a **+2** target number modifier. A magician treated without that
  modifier, or given a Deadly wound, risks **permanently losing Magic**
  *(SR3 p.129)*. A medic who takes the +2 and avoids the high-tech
  medicines is worth the extra time to a mage.
- **Biotech can't help once magical healing has been applied**
  *(SR3 p.129)*. The medic and the team's healer need to agree on who goes
  first.

## House rates

{: .house }
> These rates aren't from any SR3 book. They follow the
> [house-rule framework](../#the-house-rule-framework) that the other
> specialist pages use, and are scaled to match them.

### Base day rate, by Biotech

| Tier | Biotech | Base day rate | Typical kit |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Corpsman | 1–3 | 200–400¥ | A medkit and a couple of trauma patches |
| Paramedic | 4–5 | 800–2,000¥ | A good medkit, slap patches, maybe ex-DocWagon |
| Trauma specialist | 6–7 | 2,500–5,000¥ | A stabilization unit, combat experience |
| Elite | 8+ | 8,000¥ and up | A deluxe stabilization unit, and a surgeon's hands under fire |

### Threat multiplier

| Work | Multiplier |
| :--- | :---: |
| On call or waiting at the safehouse | ×1 |
| With the team on a run, light opposition expected | ×2 |
| Pulling casualties out under fire (a High Threat Response, in DocWagon terms) | ×3–4 |

### Hazard pay and expenses

- **Off-site**, at the safehouse or the van: no hazard pay.
- **On-site** with the team: **+100%**.
- **Consumables** at book price: every trauma patch and medkit restock used
  is billed on top.
- **Or bill per patient**, at the SR3 p.128 paramedic scale. A medic hired
  for the night and kept off-site may prefer that.

## Worked examples

| Job | House rate | Book comparison |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Paramedic (base 1,500¥) on call at the safehouse, ×1, two trauma patches used | 1,500 + 2 × 500 = **2,500¥** | Security duty: 200¥ a day |
| Trauma specialist (base 4,000¥) on-site, ×2, +100% on-site, three trauma patches used | 4,000 × 2 × 2 = 16,000, + 1,500 = **17,500¥** | A share of the run's fee |
| Billed per patient by the book: one Deadly and two Serious wounds | 400 + 2 × 200 = **800¥** | SR3 p.128 |
