### What the mage is risking

- **Drain**: casting above your Magic makes it **Physical**, and so does
  casting while astrally projecting. *(SR3 p.162)*
- **Magic loss**: on a Deadly wound, or medical treatment without the
  Awakened modifier, roll 2D6. At or under your Magic, you lose a point
  permanently. *(SR3 p.129)*
- **Astral combat**: wounds taken on the astral appear on the body. Being
  disrupted forces a Magic Loss check. *(SR3 p.175)*
- **The law**: magic of **Force 3+** is regulated in the UCAS and CAS, and a
  felony committed with magic is treated as premeditated. *(MitS p.11)*

## House rates

{: .house }
> These rates aren't from any SR3 book. They are the original gist's
> numbers, kept for convenience.

### Base day rate, by Sorcery or Conjuring

| Tier | Skill | Base day rate | Typical capability |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Hedge wizard | 1–3 | 500–1,000¥ | An aspected or weak caster with utility spells |
| Wage mage | 4–5 | 2,500–5,000¥ | A full magician: Manabolt, Armor, Fireball at Force 4–6, watcher spirits |
| Veteran | 6–7 | 6,000–12,000¥ | An initiate (grade 1–3) with a sustaining focus and Force 6+ elementals |
| Elite | 8+ | 20,000¥ and up | A high-grade initiate with metamagic (centering, shielding, reflecting) and a strong set of foci |

### Threat multiplier

| Work | Multiplier |
| :--- | :---: |
| Utility magic: Invisibility, Levitate, healing, assensing | ×1 |
| Combat spells, breaking basic wards, mid-Force spirits | ×2–3 |
| Corporate security mages, high-Force elementals, strong wards | ×4–6 |

### Hazard pay and expenses

- **Off-site** ritual work or astral recon from a circle: no hazard pay.
- **On-site** with the team: **+100%**. Mages are the first target.
- Conjuring and ritual materials are paid **up front** by the employer.
  *(Prices: MitS p.169)*
- **Bound spirit services** brought to the job: 1,000–3,000¥ per service.
