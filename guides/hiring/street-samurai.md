## House rates

{: .house }
> These rates aren't from any SR3 book. They are the original gist's
> numbers, kept for convenience.

### Base day rate, by combat skill and augmentation

| Tier | Skill and chrome | Base day rate | Typical kit |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Street tough | Skill 1–3, little or no chrome | 200–500¥ | A heavy pistol or shotgun |
| Professional | Skill 4–5, Wired 1 or Boosted 1–2 | 1,000–2,500¥ | Smartlink, SMG or assault rifle, dermal plating |
| Veteran | Skill 6–7, Wired 2 | 3,500–7,000¥ | Alpha or bioware suite, muscle replacement or augmentation, heavy armor |
| Elite | Skill 8+, Wired 3 or move-by-wire | 10,000¥ and up | Alpha or betaware, bone lacing, anti-vehicle weapons |

### Threat multiplier

| Work | Multiplier |
| :--- | :---: |
| Bodyguarding, bouncing, static guard duty | ×1 |
| A standard run: perimeter breach, corporate guards, gang turf | ×2 |
| High-threat response teams, combat drones, heavy weapons, combat mages | ×3–5 |

### Hazard pay and expenses

- Overwatch or sniper: no hazard pay.
- **Point man or breacher**: **+50% to +100%**.
- The employer supplies special ammunition (APDS, EX, gel), grenades and
  disposable launchers, and covers medical bills.
- A **medical and chrome deposit** of **2,500–10,000¥** is reasonable
  against armor-piercing or explosive fire.

## Worked examples

| Job | House rate | Companion baseline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Nightclub bodyguard, professional (base 1,500¥), ×1 | **1,500¥** a night | **200¥** a day |
| Counter-gang hit, veteran (base 5,000¥), ×2, point man +50% | 5,000 × 2 × 1.5 = **15,000¥** plus ammunition | Enforcement: 1,000¥; assassination: 5,000¥ |
| Breach against a high-threat response team, veteran-plus (base 7,000¥), ×4, breacher +50% | 7,000 × 4 × 1.5 = **42,000¥** plus ammunition and medical | A share of the extraction fee |
