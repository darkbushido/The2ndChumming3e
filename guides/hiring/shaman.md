### Why domain matters

- **No domain, no spirit.** A shaman inside a building can only summon a
  hearth spirit. In the middle of a parking garage there may be nothing to
  call at all. *(SR3 p.184)*
- **Totem modifiers** add dice to some spells and spirits and take them from
  others. Bear, for example, gets +2 dice for health spells and forest
  spirits. *(SR3 p.163–165)*
- Nature spirits **disappear at sunrise and sunset**, taking their unused
  services with them. *(SR3 p.186)*

## House rates

{: .house }
> These rates aren't from any SR3 book. They are the original gist's
> numbers, kept for convenience.

### Base day rate, by Conjuring or Sorcery

| Tier | Skill | Base day rate | Typical capability |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Hedge shaman | 1–3 | 400–800¥ | Force 1–3 spirits, basic detection and healing |
| Sprawl shaman | 4–5 | 2,500–5,000¥ | Force 4–6 city, hearth and storm spirits, a medicine lodge |
| Spirit talker | 6–7 | 7,000–13,000¥ | An initiate (grade 1–3) with masking, centering and invoking |
| Elite | 8+ | 22,000¥ and up | A high-grade initiate commanding powerful spirits |

### Threat multiplier

| Work | Multiplier |
| :--- | :---: |
| Concealment, healing, assensing, spirit scouting | ×1 |
| Combat spells, hostile wards, mid-Force spirits in a fight | ×2–3 |
| Security mages, high-Force spirits, working **outside a helpful domain** | ×4–6 |

### Hazard pay and expenses

- **Off-site**, working from the lodge: no hazard pay.
- **On-site** with the team: **+100%**.
- **Wrong-domain surcharge**: **+50%** when the job keeps the shaman away
  from spirits their totem favors.
- **Spirit services** for the team: 1,500–4,000¥ per service.
