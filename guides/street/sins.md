# SINs & the SINless
{: .no_toc }

What a System Identification Number gets you, what life without one looks
like, and how fake IDs work in SR3.

1. TOC
{:toc}

{: .fixed }
> Original gist: "Life with a SIN vs. Living SINless".
> - The **Fake SIN price table was invented**. In SR3 a fake ID is a forged
>   credstick, and its rating sets its price: **Rating × Rating × 1,000¥** at
>   ratings 1–4, rising steeply after that. The table is below.
> - A SIN is not a "12-digit alphanumeric code". The book says the number is
>   **generated from personal data**, and police can read your birth date,
>   state of origin and initials from it.
> - "30–40% of the population is SINless" appears in no SR3 book checked.
> - There's no "Lesser / Greater Magical Permit". In the UCAS and CAS, magic
>   of **Force 3 or more** is regulated and needs a permit. Unregistered
>   spells, spirits and foci fall under legality category **(T) Class E
>   Magic**.
> - Permits **require a valid SIN**, and a fake one must beat a Rating 6
>   verification system.

---

## What a SIN is  *(SR3 p.238)*

- The **UCAS** introduced SINs in **2036**, requiring every citizen to
  register. People living in the UCAS without one are "probationary
  citizens": they can't vote and have few or no civil rights.
- A SIN is normally registered **at birth**, if the birth happens in a
  standard hospital. Anyone born elsewhere is SINless unless they register
  later, and registering means proving to the government that you're worth
  admitting.
- Most governments use SINs or something like them, and they **share
  databases**. Corporations issue **corporate SINs** to their employees.
- The number is generated from **personal data**. Police can work out your
  birth date, state of origin and initials from it.

## Life with a SIN  *(SR3 p.238–239)*

You need a SIN to get a legal job, open a bank account, own property, go to
school, rent an apartment, set up utilities, and travel legally, which
includes **buying a bus ticket**. Your credstick carries your ID, licenses
and permits, and can hold your fingerprints, palmprints and retinal prints.

The cost is a **data trail**. Every legal purchase leaves credit records and
inventory, and possibly video footage, and stores that realize they sold to
shadowrunners will hand those records to the police. *(SR3 p.273)*

## Life without one  *(SR3 p.238)*

- You **officially don't exist**, which makes you hard to trace.
- If you're arrested, you may be abused, locked away or "disappeared", with
  no data trail to prove you existed. More often you're issued a **criminal
  SIN**, which is yours for life and is stored in police databases with your
  photograph, fingerprints and perhaps more.
- Legal work, housing and travel are all closed to you.

Players decide at character creation whether their character has a SIN.
*(SR3 p.238)*

## Credsticks  *(SR3 p.238–239)*

A credstick is a pen-sized tube that serves as **ID and credit card
together**.

| Credstick | Transaction limit | ID required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Standard | 1–5,000¥ | Passcode |
| Silver | 1–20,000¥ | Fingerprint |
| Gold | 1–200,000¥ | Voiceprint |
| Platinum | 1–1,000,000¥ | Retinal scan |
| Ebony | Unlimited | Cellular scan |

A **certified credstick** is registered to no one. It's worth what's loaded
on it, needs no ID, and can't be used as ID. It's the shadowrunner's usual
way to get paid. *(SR3 p.239)*

## Fake IDs: forging credsticks  *(SR3 p.239)*

Faking an identity means inserting a believable credit history into
databases all over the world. Only well-connected forgery outfits, often
based in data havens, can do it. At least **half the cost is paid up
front** to the fixer.

### Creating a Credstick Table  *(SR3 p.239)*

| Stick rating | Cost | Availability | Street Index |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| 1–4 | Rating × Rating × 1,000¥ | Rating / 24 hours | 1 |
| 5–8 | Rating × 5,000¥ | Rating / 72 hours | 1 |
| 9–12 | Rating × 10,000¥ | Rating / 14 days | 1 |
| 13+ | Rating × 50,000¥ | Rating / 30 days | 1 |

For example, a Rating 4 ID costs **16,000¥**, a Rating 6 costs **30,000¥**,
and a Rating 10 costs **100,000¥**.

**Using a fake**: make an **Opposed Test** of the stick's rating against
the rating of the verification system. The side with more successes wins. On
a **tie**, the operator is told to question the bearer about their history,
and one wrong answer gets the ID rejected. *(SR3 p.239)*

## Permits and licenses  *(SR3 p.273–274)*
{: #permits-and-licenses }

- Gear whose Legality Code includes a **"P"** can be legally permitted.
  Applying takes a **valid SIN**, and may need proof of employment and a good
  reason. *(SR3 p.274)*
- Test **Etiquette against the item's Availability + 2**. The base time is
  the item's, divided by your successes. *(SR3 p.274)*
- A permit to **possess** usually costs **5%** of the item's price. A permit
  to **possess and transport** costs **10%**. *(SR3 p.274)*
- **Criminal-SIN holders and the SINless can't get permits.** Applying with
  a fake ID means it must beat a **Rating 6** verification system. *(SR3
  p.274)*
- Holding the right permit gives **−2 to the item's Availability** when
  you're buying on the street. *(SR3 p.274)*

### Magic and the law

- In the UCAS and CAS, **any spell, spirit, focus or other magical effect of
  Force 3 or higher is legally regulated**, though permits can be had. A
  felony committed with magic always counts as **premeditated**. *(MitS p.11)*
- On the Local Fines and Punishment Table, **(T) Class E Magic** covers
  unregistered spells, spirits and foci. *(SR3 p.274)*
- Reading astral signatures is accepted forensic evidence, like fingerprints
  or DNA. *(MitS p.11)*

{: .note }
> **Runner practice.** Carrying several fake IDs for different purposes is
> common in play: one for the safehouse lease, one carrying permits, one to
> burn. The book doesn't require it, but a burned ID is expensive to replace,
> so spreading the risk makes sense.
