Reading a spell (SR3 p.178, p.191)

Each spell in the Street Grimoire is given as Type · Target · Duration · Drain. Its category is the section it’s listed in.

Statistic Values What it means
Type M (mana) or P (physical) Physical spells affect physical properties, and only they affect non-living objects. Mana spells affect minds, spirits, life force and magic, and only they affect astral forms (SR3 p.178).
Target W, B, I, OR, or a number The target number. W, B and I are the target’s Willpower, Body and Intelligence; OR is the Object Resistance Table (SR3 p.182). An unwilling target also makes a Spell Resistance Test (SR3 p.191).
Duration I, S or P Instant, Sustained or Permanent. See Sustaining spells (SR3 p.178).
Drain e.g. +1(M) A modifier to the Drain Power, which is half the Force rounded down, and the Drain Level (SR3 p.191).

Range. Most spells are line of sight: if the caster can see the target, at any distance, it can be affected. Some, especially health spells, need a touch. Touching an unwilling target takes an unarmed attack, at −1 TN, as part of the spellcasting; one net success is enough (SR3 p.178). Area spells affect everything within the caster’s Magic in meters, friend and foe, including the caster (SR3 p.181).

Variable Drain. Spells that do or heal damage have a Drain Level that depends on the damage. If a modifier would push the Drain Level past Deadly, each level past Deadly adds +2 to the Drain Power instead (SR3 p.191).

Example. A Force 6 Fireball cast at Serious has Drain +1(Damage Level +2). Power: 6 ÷ 2 = 3, + 1 = 4. Level: Serious + 2 is one level past Deadly, so the level is Deadly and the Power rises by 2, to 6. The caster resists 6D Drain.

Permanent spells (SR3 p.178)

A permanent spell must be sustained for a base time, set by its Drain Level, before its effect becomes permanent. Sorcery successes can be spent dividing that time instead of on the effect.

Drain Level Base time to become permanent
Light 5 Turns
Moderate 10 Turns
Serious 15 Turns
Deadly 20 Turns

Starting spells: Spell Points (SR3 p.55, p.60)

  • A starting full magician gets 25 Spell Points; an aspected magician gets 35. Adepts get none.
  • A spell costs 1 Spell Point per point of Force.
  • Spell Points can also bond foci, at 1 Spell Point per point of Karma, and buy starting spirits: 1 per point of Force plus 2 per service owed.
  • More Spell Points cost 25,000¥ each, up to 50 in all, and only at character creation. They can’t be sold back.
  • No starting spell, focus or spirit can be above Force 6.

After character creation, spells are learned with Good Karma.

Learning a spell (SR3 p.180)

You learn from a teacher or a spell formula, of your own tradition.

   
Where you study A shaman needs a lodge, a mage a Sorcery library, rated at least the desired Force.
A teacher Typically 1,000¥ × the Force, plus living expenses. They can teach up to the Force they know. With the Instruction skill, every 2 successes on an Instruction (4) Test add a die to your test.
A formula Bought from a talismonger. For a spell with variable Drain, price it at its highest Drain.
The test Sorcery, with Magic Background as a Complementary Skill, against twice the desired Force. Shamans get totem modifiers; injury and sustaining modifiers apply.
The time Days equal to the Force, divided by the successes; at least one day.
The Karma Good Karma equal to the Force.
Failure No successes wastes days equal to the Force. Failed study costs no Karma, but the teacher still expects to be paid.

To raise a spell’s Force you learn it again at the new Force (SR3 p.178), at the full cost.

Spell formula prices (SR3 p.305)

Drain Availability Cost Street Index
L Force / 24 hrs Force × 50¥ 2
M Force / 48 hrs Force × 100¥ 2
S Force / 72 hrs Force × 500¥ 2
D Force / 7 days Force × 1,000¥ 2

Worked example. A mage learns Manabolt at Force 5 from a formula. Manabolt’s Drain is the damage level, so the formula is priced at its highest, D: 5 × 1,000¥ = 5,000¥, availability 5 / 7 days. She needs a Sorcery library of rating 5 or more. Her Sorcery Test is against 5 × 2 = TN 10. With 1 success it takes 5 ÷ 1 = 5 days, and it costs 5 Good Karma.

Limited spells (SR3 p.180–181)

A limit makes a spell easier, in one of two ways, chosen when you learn it: it either lowers the Force for Drain (including whether the Drain is Physical), or lowers the Karma cost and the learning TN, by its modifier.

Limit Modifier The catch
Fetish −1 The fetish, an enchanted reusable object for one spell category, must be touching you to cast. Losing it means attuning a new one: hours of meditation equal to the Force.
Exclusive −2 Casting and sustaining it is an Exclusive Action (p.160).

A spell can have both, if they’re for different purposes. An unlimited version has to be learned separately.

The book’s example (SR3 p.181): Grandfather Bones, Magic 6, learns Fireball at Force 8 with the exclusive limit. Taken off Drain, he treats it as Force 6 for Drain, so the Drain isn’t Physical. Taken off learning instead, his TN is 8 × 2 − 2 = 14 and it costs 6 Karma.

Illusion spells (SR3 p.195–196)

Illusions can’t do lasting harm. Mana illusions fool minds, not cameras; physical illusions fool sensors too. Equal or more successes on the Resistance Test see through it. Directed mana illusions are resisted by Willpower and physical ones by Intelligence; indirect illusions are all resisted by Intelligence.

Manipulation spells (SR3 p.196–198)

Many have a Threshold: after the Resistance Test, the caster needs at least that many successes, or the spell fails.

Control manipulations are resisted by Willpower, with a Threshold of half the target’s Willpower, rounded down.

Spell Type Target Duration Drain Notes
Control Actions M W S +1(M) Puppet the target’s body, at +4 to its TNs.
Control Emotion M W S +1(M) An overwhelming emotion of the caster’s choice.
Control Thoughts M W S +1(S) Command the target’s mind with a Simple Action.
Influence M W P S One post-hypnotic suggestion.

Doing it in The 2nd Chumming (Foundry)

Getting a spell onto the sheet

Drag a spell onto the character from the compendiums (Shadowrun 3rd, Magic in the Shadows and State of the Art 2064 under Spells), and it appears on the Magic tab with a Cast button. Set its learned Force on the spell. A spell with a Force can be cast at that Force or lower, never higher (SR3 p.178). A spell with no Force recorded isn’t capped, and the cast dialog says so.

Learning with Karma

On the Bio tab, Spend Karma… lists each spell on the sheet under Magic. Pick one, enter the Force to learn it at, and any limit modifier taken off the cost. It charges Good Karma equal to the Force, less the limit (SR3 p.180–181), and records the new Force. The purchase goes into the 📒 Ledger, and the GM can undo it there.

The Sorcery Test to learn, the days of study, and the teacher’s or formula’s price are the GM’s to run. A limit taken off Drain instead is recorded on the spell, and the cast drains at the lowered Force (SR3 p.180).

Casting

Cast opens the Force dialog, then the target, then Spell Pool, and rolls. Every step after that, from resistance to Drain, is on Spellcasting, Resistance & Drain.
