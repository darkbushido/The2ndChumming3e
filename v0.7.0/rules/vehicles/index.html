Vehicles

Reading a vehicle’s statistics (SR3 p.130–133)

Statistic What it means
Handling The base TN for Driving Tests, so lower is better. Most ground vehicles show two: on road / off road, as in 3/8.
Speed Maximum safe speed, in meters per Combat Turn. A fixed-wing aircraft has two, its minimum and its maximum.
Accel How much each success on a Driving Test can add to Speed in a chase.
Body Toughness. It also sets the number of weapon mounts.
Armor Hardened armor: it stops any hit whose Power (after the vehicle’s Body, but not counting burst or autofire) doesn’t beat it.
Sig Signature: the TN for sensors and guided weapons to lock on.
Autonav The collision-avoidance and navigation system, a driver’s assistant. It can’t fire weapons.
Pilot A drone’s own brain. Ordinary vehicles have none (—).
Sensor Detection, identification and targeting systems.
Cargo Cargo Factor: each point is a cube half a meter on a side.
Load What it can carry, in kilograms.

The full rules for each are summarized on Rigging.

Rigging a vehicle (SR3 p.306)

Gear Cost What it does
Datajack port 2,500¥ (5,000¥ for a bike) Drive by datajack: +1 Reaction for Vehicle Tests and vehicle combat. Not enough for a VCR. Uses 1 Cargo Factor and 15 kg of Load.
Remote-control interface (“Remote Control Gear”) 2,500¥ × Body Lets the vehicle join a rigger’s remote-control network as a drone, with a Pilot Rating of 1. Needs Sensor 1 or more. Uses no cargo space.
Rigger adaptation (“Vehicle Control Gear”) 2,500¥ The “black box” that lets a character with a VCR rig it. Includes a datajack port; needs Sensor 1 or more. Uses 1 Cargo Factor and 10 kg of Load.

A Vehicle Control Rig itself is cyberware: each level adds +2 Reaction and an Initiative die while rigging, and gives the Control Pool (SR3 p.44, p.301). See Rigging.

Weapon mounts (SR3 p.307, p.312)

  • Hardpoints carry heavy and vehicle weapons (MMGs and larger); firmpoints carry LMGs, assault rifles and smaller.
  • A vehicle’s Body sets how many it has: a hardpoint takes 2 points of Body, a firmpoint 1. A Body 4 vehicle could have 2 hardpoints, or a hardpoint and 2 firmpoints, or 4 firmpoints.
  • Firmpoints are fixed mounts or pintle mounts; hardpoints are fixed mounts or turrets.
  • Fixed mounts fire in a fixed arc, usually forward or back, and halve recoil, which cancels a heavy weapon’s doubling. The driving rigger can fire them. A side-firing fixed mount adds its recoil to the vehicle’s Handling and forces a Crash Test. External fixed mounts lower the Signature by 1.

Doing it in The 2nd Chumming (Foundry)

A vehicle is its own actor with its own sheet. Everything about using one is on the Rigging page:

  • Getting a vehicle onto a character’s sheet: drag it from the compendiums (Vehicles for Shadowrun 3rd, Rigger 3 and State of the Art), or + Add Vehicle on the Vehicles tab.
  • Jacking in: VCR / RCD / Auto.
  • Driving without rigging.
  • Driving Tests and Crash Tests.
  • The Chase Scene.

Mounted weapons go on the vehicle sheet’s Weapons tab. They don’t use clips or reloads.
