What they’re up against

  • Weapon detection: MAD scanners are rated 1–4 hand-held and 4–9 built into doorways. The scanner rolls its rating against the weapon’s Concealability. (SR3 p.237)
  • Cyberware scanners are rated 3–9, against TN 3, or 6 for alphaware. (SR3 p.237)
  • Astral security: bound spirits, wards and dual-natured watch critters. (SR3 p.237)

House rates

These rates aren’t from any SR3 book. They are the original gist’s numbers, kept for convenience.

Base day rate, by Stealth, Electronics and similar skills

Tier Skill Base day rate Typical kit
Cat burglar 1–3 250–500¥ Lockpicks and climbing gear
Professional 4–5 1,500–3,000¥ Maglock passkey, sequencer, voice modulator, dermal sheath
Veteran 6–7 4,000–8,000¥ High-grade electronics, muscle replacement, retractable climbing claws
Elite 8+ 12,000¥ and up Ruthenium-polymer camouflage and a full stealth suite

Threat multiplier

Target Multiplier
Commercial or residential: locks, basic maglocks, cameras ×1
Corporate facility: patrols, pressure and laser sensors, better maglocks ×2–3
Black site or military storage: monowire, turrets, wards, instant response ×4–5

Hazard pay and expenses

  • Solo entry, or opening the door for the team: no hazard pay.
  • Leading the team through the sensors under fire: +50% to +100%.
  • The employer pays for consumables such as bypass cards, thermite and fiber-optic cameras.
  • A gear deposit of 25–50% of the value of expensive kit taken into high-danger areas.
