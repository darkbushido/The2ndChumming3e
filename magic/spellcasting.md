# Spellcasting, Resistance & Drain
{: .no_toc }

How a spell is cast, step by step and in the order the book gives: who
rolls, what they roll, and what happens. All rules are from the core
rulebook, *(SR3 p.178–185, p.191)*.

1. TOC
{:toc}

---

## Who rolls what

| Role | Roll | Against | When |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Caster** | **Sorcery** + Spell Pool (no more pool dice than Sorcery dice) | The target's attribute, or the spell's listed TN | Step 3 |
| **Target** | The spell's **target attribute**: Willpower, Body or Intelligence | The spell's **Force** | Step 4 |
| **A protecting magician** | **Spell Defense** dice: Sorcery + Spell Pool set aside earlier | The spell's **Force** | Step 4 |
| **Caster** | **Willpower** + any number of Spell Pool dice | **Drain Power**: Force ÷ 2 + modifier | Step 6 |
| **GM** | Rolls the Sorcery Test **secretly** for detection spells | | Step 3 |

**Spell Pool** = (Intelligence + Willpower + Magic) ÷ 3, rounded down. It
refreshes every Combat Turn and is shared between casting, Spell Defense and
Drain. *(SR3 p.44, p.180)*

---

## The sequence at a glance  *(SR3 p.181)*

The book's own six steps, all within your one Complex Action:

| # | Step | What you decide or roll |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [Preparation](#step-1-preparation) | Spell, Force, Damage Level, dice split, area radius |
| 2 | [Targeting](#step-2-targeting) | Can you see it? Is it on your plane? |
| 3 | [Sorcery Test](#step-3-sorcery-test) | Caster: Sorcery + Spell Pool vs the target number |
| 4 | [Spell Resistance](#step-4-spell-resistance) | Target: attribute vs Force; plus any Spell Defense |
| 5 | [Spell Effect](#step-5-spell-effect) | Net successes decide the effect |
| 6 | [Drain](#step-6-drain) | Caster: Willpower + Spell Pool vs Drain Power |

## Step 1: Preparation  *(SR3 p.178, p.181, p.191)*
{: #step-1-preparation }

Decide all of these before rolling.

- **The spell and its Force.** You can cast at the Force you learned it at
  or lower, never higher. The minimum is Force 1. *(SR3 p.178)* Higher Force
  is harder to resist and produces more Drain.
- **Damage Level, for combat and elemental spells.** You choose **L, M, S or
  D** when you cast. That choice is both the spell's base damage **and** its
  base Drain Level. *(SR3 p.191, p.196)*
- **How many Sorcery dice** go into this spell, and how many **Spell Pool**
  dice. You can't add more pool dice than Sorcery dice. *(SR3 p.180)*
- **More than one spell?** You can split Sorcery and Spell Pool dice to cast
  several spells in one Complex Action, up to your Sorcery rating in spells,
  with at least 1 Sorcery die each. Each spell then gets **+2 to its Drain
  TN for every extra spell**. Two spells means +2 on each Drain Test; three
  means +4 on each. *(SR3 p.181)*
- **Sustaining anything?** Choose whether to keep or drop spells you're
  already sustaining. Each one you keep costs **+2 to all your tests**,
  including this Sorcery Test and its Drain Test. *(SR3 p.178)*
- **Area spells**: the base radius is your **Magic in meters**, and it hits
  everyone valid inside, **including you and your friends**. Withhold
  Sorcery dice to change it: **every 2 dice withheld shrink it by 1 m**, or
  **every 1 die withheld widens it by 1 m**. *(SR3 p.181)*

## Step 2: Targeting  *(SR3 p.181–182)*
{: #step-2-targeting }

- You must **see** the target, and be **on the same plane** (physical or
  astral). Glasses, mirrors, fiber optics and cybereyes are fine. **Any
  digital image**, such as a camera feed, simsense or a vision-altering spell
  like Clairvoyance, is not. *(SR3 p.181)*
- **Glass** doesn't stop most spells, because the effect happens at the
  target and nothing travels. An **opaque** barrier blocks the spell.
  *(SR3 p.182)*
- **Touch-range spells** skip cover and visibility modifiers, but you must
  touch the target first with an Unarmed Combat attack at −1 TN, as part of
  the same action. One net success is enough. *(SR3 p.178, p.183)*
- **From the astral**, you can only cast **mana** spells, and only at astral
  targets. **Astrally perceiving** casters can target both planes, but can be
  attacked from both. *(SR3 p.182)*

## Step 3: Sorcery Test  *(SR3 p.182–183)*
{: #step-3-sorcery-test }

**The caster rolls Sorcery dice plus Spell Pool dice.**

### Target number

| Target | Base TN |
| :--- | :--- |
| A living being, **mana** spell | The target's **Willpower** |
| A living being, **physical** spell | The target's **Body** |
| An object | The **Object Resistance Table**, below |
| Spells with a fixed number | As listed in the spell, e.g. "Target: 4" |

#### Object Resistance Table  *(SR3 p.182)*

| Material | TN |
| :--- | :---: |
| Natural objects: trees, soil, unprocessed water | 3 |
| Low-tech manufactured: brick, leather, simple plastics | 5 |
| High-tech manufactured: advanced plastics, alloys, electronics | 8 |
| Highly processed: computers, complex toxic waste | 10+ |

To affect an object at all, the spell's **Force must be at least half its
Object Resistance**, rounded down. Vehicles add their Body and half their
armor to Object Resistance before halving. *(SR3 p.182)*

### Modifiers to the Sorcery TN  *(SR3 p.182–183)*

- **Cover and visibility**, as for Perception.
- **Injury modifiers** (+1 / +2 / +3).
- **+2 per spell you're sustaining.**
- **An astral barrier** you're casting across, such as a ward, hermetic
  circle or shamanic lodge, adds **its Force**. Its creator is exempt.

### Results

- **No successes:** the spell fails.
- **All 1s:** it fails **and** your Drain TN goes up by **+2**. *(SR3 p.182)*
- **Area spells:** roll once and compare the dice against **each** target's
  TN separately. Each target then resists separately. *(SR3 p.182)*
- **Detection spells:** the GM rolls in secret. On all 1s, the GM gives you
  false information. *(SR3 p.182)*

## Step 4: Spell Resistance  *(SR3 p.183)*
{: #step-4-spell-resistance }

Two different things can reduce the caster's successes here. Both can happen
against the same spell.

### The target resists

- Any **living** target that isn't **willing** makes a **Spell Resistance
  Test**: its **targeted attribute** (usually Body, Intelligence or
  Willpower) against **TN = the spell's Force**.
- **No modifiers apply** unless the spell says so. That includes the
  target's wounds.
- **Non-living, non-magical** targets don't resist.

### A magician blocks it: Spell Defense  *(SR3 p.183)*

- At some earlier point, a magician **set aside Sorcery and Spell Pool dice
  for defense**. That's a Free Action.
- They can protect up to **their Sorcery rating** in subjects, all within
  **Magic × 100 m** and on the same plane. Protecting yourself counts as one.
- When a protected subject is targeted, the defender **senses it** and may
  roll any of those dice against **TN = the spell's Force**. It takes no
  action.
- **Every success subtracts one success from the caster's Sorcery Test.** If
  that takes the caster to **0 or less**, the spell fails.
- Dice used are **gone until the next Combat Turn**.

## Step 5: Spell Effect  *(SR3 p.183, p.191)*
{: #step-5-spell-effect }

Compare the caster's remaining successes with the target's resistance
successes.

- **Target ties or wins:** **no effect.** A tie goes to the target.
- **Caster wins:** the **net successes** (caster − target) measure the
  effect. The spell's description says what they buy.
- **No resistance roll** (an object, or a willing subject): all the caster's
  successes count, and **1 success always gives some effect**.
- **Damaging spells:** the chosen Damage Level **stages up one level per 2
  net successes**, up to Deadly.

### Combat spells: armor doesn't help  *(SR3 p.191)*

Combat spells (Manabolt, Powerbolt, Stunbolt and their ball versions)
damage the target **directly and bypass armor**. The Spell Resistance Test
is the target's only defense, so **there's no Damage Resistance Test**.
Mana spells are resisted with **Willpower** and affect only living and
magical targets. Physical spells are resisted with **Body** and can hit
objects too.

| Spell | Type | Resisted by | Drain | Damage |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| Stunbolt / Stunball | Mana | Willpower | −1(Damage Level) / −1(Damage Level +1) | Stun |
| Manabolt / Manaball | Mana | Willpower | (Damage Level) / (Damage Level +1) | Physical |
| Powerbolt / Powerball | Physical | Body | +1(Damage Level) / +1(Damage Level +1) | Physical |
| Death Touch | Mana | Willpower | (Damage Level −1) | Physical, by touch |

### Elemental spells work like guns  *(SR3 p.183, p.196)*

Fireball, Flamethrower, Lightning Bolt, Acid Stream and similar spells make
**real fire, lightning or acid**. They follow the ranged combat rules,
so steps 3 to 5 change:

1. **Sorcery is the ranged combat skill.** The base TN is **4 at any range**
   if you can see the target, plus cover, visibility, injury and sustaining
   modifiers. Spell Pool dice can be added. *(SR3 p.183)*
2. The target can **dodge** with Combat Pool. *(SR3 p.183)*
3. Instead of a Spell Resistance Test, the target makes a **Damage Resistance
   Test** with Body plus Combat Pool dice, and **Impact armor at half its
   rating** (rounded down) protects. *(SR3 p.183, p.196)*
4. The chosen Damage Level stages up 2 successes per level for the caster
   and down for the target, as in ordinary ranged combat. *(SR3 p.183)*
5. **Secondary effects** can still set fires, cook off ammunition or melt
   material, even if the primary damage is staged to nothing. *(SR3 p.196)*

Glass and walls **do** block elemental spells, which have to break through
like bullets. Targets hidden behind cover inside a Fireball's radius **still
get hit**. Spell Defense and astral barriers still work against them.
*(SR3 p.182)*

{: .note }
> **The damage Power of an elemental spell.** The core book gives the spell's
> Damage Code but never states its Power in so many words. The usual reading
> is that **the Power is the spell's Force**, so a Force 6 Fireball cast at
> Moderate is 6M before staging.

## Step 6: Drain  *(SR3 p.162, p.180, p.183, p.191)*
{: #step-6-drain }

Right after casting, in the same action, **whether or not the spell worked**,
the caster resists Drain.

### Reading a Drain code  *(SR3 p.191)*

A spell's Drain looks like **`+1(M)`** or **`-1(Damage Level +1)`**:

- The number **outside** the brackets modifies the **Power**, which is the
  **target number**:
  **Drain Power = Force ÷ 2, rounded down, + that modifier.**
- The part **inside** the brackets is the **Drain Level**, the damage you
  take before resisting. For combat and elemental spells it's the Damage
  Level you **chose**, adjusted by any modifier written with it.
- If a modifier would push the Drain Level **above Deadly**, add **+2 Drain
  Power per level above** instead.

### Adding up the TN

| Source | Change |
| :--- | :--- |
| Base | Force ÷ 2 (round down) + the spell's modifier |
| Each spell you're sustaining | +2 *(p.178, p.180)* |
| Each extra spell cast in the same action | +2 *(p.181)* |
| The Sorcery Test came up all 1s | +2 *(p.182)* |

Otherwise, **no modifiers apply**, including wounds. *(SR3 p.183)*

### The roll

**The caster rolls Willpower plus any number of Spell Pool dice.** There's no
cap here, unlike the Sorcery Test. **Every 2 successes lower the Drain Level
one step.** Below Light, you take nothing. *(SR3 p.44, p.183)*

### Stun or Physical?  *(SR3 p.162, p.183)*

- **Stun** normally.
- **Physical** if the **Force is higher than your Magic**, or if you're
  **astrally projecting**, whatever the Force.
- A **limited spell** (fetish −1, exclusive −2) can lower the Force *for
  Drain purposes*, both for the TN and for this Stun-or-Physical check.
  *(SR3 p.180)*
- **Physical damage from Drain can't be healed by magic**, only by rest and
  medical care. *(SR3 p.162)*

## Sustaining spells  *(SR3 p.178)*

| Duration | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Instant (I)** | Takes effect and ends at once. The results, such as wounds, stay. Combat spells work this way. |
| **Sustained (S)** | Lasts while you concentrate, which takes no action. |
| **Permanent (P)** | Must be sustained for a base time, then becomes permanent. Sorcery successes can be spent dividing that time instead of on the effect. |

While sustaining:

- **+2 to all your tests per sustained spell**, including Drain Resistance
  but not ordinary Damage Resistance.
- You can sustain up to **your Sorcery rating** in spells at once.
- **If you take damage**, roll **Sorcery against the spell's Force** (plus
  injury modifiers) to keep it going.
- Dropping a spell is a **Free Action**. Being knocked out or falling asleep
  drops them all.
- An **Exclusive Action** means dropping every sustained spell first.

A **sustaining focus** holds a spell for you, and the +2 doesn't apply to
you. *(SR3 p.191)*

## Worked example: Manabolt

Kestrel has **Magic 6**, **Sorcery 5**, and a Spell Pool of **4**
(Intelligence 5, Willpower 5, Magic 6 → 16 ÷ 3 = 5, and she spent 1 earlier
this turn). She casts **Manabolt at Force 5**, choosing **Serious**, at a
guard with Willpower **4**. She isn't sustaining anything.

1. **Preparation.** Force 5, Serious, 5 Sorcery dice plus **3** Spell Pool
   dice. That leaves 1 pool die for Drain.
2. **Targeting.** She can see him and they're both on the physical plane.
3. **Sorcery Test.** 8 dice against **TN 4** (his Willpower). She gets **4
   successes**.
4. **Resistance.** The guard rolls Willpower 4 against **TN 5** (the Force)
   and gets **1**. Nobody is running Spell Defense for him.
5. **Effect.** 4 − 1 = **3 net successes**. Two of them stage Serious up to
   **Deadly**; the third is left over. Combat spells ignore armor, so the
   guard takes a **Deadly Physical** wound.
6. **Drain.** Manabolt's Drain is `(Damage Level)`. Power is 5 ÷ 2 = **2**,
   and the level is **Serious**, the level she *chose*, not the staged one.
   She rolls Willpower 5 plus her last Spell Pool die, 6 dice against TN
   **2**, and gets **3 successes**. Two of them take Serious down to
   **Moderate**. Force 5 isn't higher than her Magic 6, so it's **Moderate
   Stun**.

**If she had cast at Force 7** (assuming she'd learned it that high):
Drain Power would be 3, and the Drain would be **Physical**, because 7 is
higher than her Magic.

**If a mage were protecting the guard** with Spell Defense and rolled 2
successes against TN 5, Kestrel's 4 would drop to **2**. The guard's 1
resistance success would leave **1 net success**: not enough to stage up,
so he'd take **Serious**.
