### What the rigger is risking

- **Their body.** A jumped-in rigger resists **6M** Physical when the vehicle
  takes Serious damage and **6S** when it's destroyed, using **Willpower**
  with no pool dice. *(SR3 p.145)*
- **Their implants.** A Vehicle Control Rig costs *(SR3 p.302)*:

| VCR | Essence | Cost |
| :---: | :---: | ---: |
| Level 1 | 2 | 12,000¥ |
| Level 2 | 3 | 60,000¥ |
| Level 3 | 5 | 300,000¥ |

- **Their vehicles.** Whatever is shot down comes out of the rigger's pocket
  unless the contract says otherwise.

## House rates

{: .house }
> These rates aren't from any SR3 book. They are the original gist's
> numbers, kept for convenience.

### Base day rate, by vehicle or gunnery skill and VCR

| Tier | Skill and VCR | Base day rate | Typical hardware |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Amateur | Skill 1–3, no VCR or VCR 1 | 300–600¥ | An ordinary ground vehicle; no combat drones |
| Professional | Skill 4–5, VCR 1–2 | 1,500–3,000¥ | An upgraded getaway car and one or two surveillance drones |
| Veteran | Skill 6–7, VCR 2–3 | 4,000–8,000¥ | An armored vehicle, a remote control deck, and combat drones such as a Doberman or Steel Lynx |
| Elite | Skill 8+, VCR 3 | 12,000¥ and up | Security or military aircraft, heavy weapons, a drone swarm |

### Threat multiplier

| Work | Multiplier |
| :--- | :---: |
| Transport or recon, avoiding trouble | ×1 |
| Combat support or a getaway under fire | ×2 |
| Heavy combat, anti-vehicle fire or assault | ×4–6 |

### Hazard pay and expenses

- **Remote** (deck or autonav): no hazard pay.
- **Jumped in** with a VCR: **+50%**, because the rigger feels Serious and
  Destroyed damage.
- The employer pays for ammunition, fuel and expended countermeasures.
- For high-threat work, a **deposit of 25–50%** of each drone's replacement
  value.

## Worked examples

| Job | House rate | Companion baseline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Getaway driver, professional (base 1,500¥), ×2 | 1,500 × 2 = **3,000¥** plus fuel | Courier run: 1,000¥ |
| Drone surveillance, veteran (base 5,000¥), remote, ×1 | **5,000¥** plus lost drones | Investigation: 200¥ a day |
| Fire support on an assault, veteran, ×4, jumped in +50% | 5,000 × 4 × 1.5 = **30,000¥** | A share of the destruction or extraction fee |
