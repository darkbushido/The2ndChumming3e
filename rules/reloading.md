# Reloading
{: .no_toc }

How each kind of gun is reloaded, what it costs in actions, and buying and
sharing ammunition. The core rule is the **Ammo Reloading Table**, *(SR3
p.280)*.

1. TOC
{:toc}

## Reading the ammo code  *(SR3 p.280)*

Every firearm's **Ammo** entry is a capacity followed by a letter in
brackets. The letter is how the gun is reloaded.

| Code | Method | How it works | Example *(SR3 p.278–279)* |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **(c)** | Removable clip | A detachable clip holding many rounds. The most common method. | Ares Predator, `15 (c)` |
| **(b)** | Break action | The gun hinges open and rounds go in by hand. | Walther Palm Pistol, `2 (b)` |
| **(m)** | Internal magazine | Rounds are pushed into a port inside the gun. | Remington Roomsweeper, `8 (m)` |
| **(cy)** | Cylinder | A pop-out cylinder, loaded by hand or with a speed loader. | Ruger Super Warhawk, `6 (cy)` |
| **(belt)** | Belt feed | A long belt of linked rounds fed continuously. | Ingram Valiant LMG, `50 (c)/Belt` |

## Ammo Reloading Table  *(SR3 p.280)*

| Reloading method | Action required | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Removable clip (c)** | Simple Action | Remove **or** insert a clip |
| | Complex Action | Load **(Quickness)** rounds into a clip |
| **Break action (b)** | Complex Action | Load **2** rounds |
| **Internal magazine (m)** | Complex Action | Load **(Quickness)** rounds |
| **Cylinder (cy)** | Complex Action | Load **(Quickness)** rounds |
| | Complex Action | Use a speed loader |
| **Belt feed (belt)** | Complex Action | Insert a belt |
| | Complex Action | Load **(Quickness × 2)** rounds into a belt |

"(Quickness) rounds" means **as many rounds as your Quickness** per action.
A character with Quickness 5 loads 5 rounds with each Complex Action.

## Clips: the two-step swap  *(SR3 p.105–107)*

Changing a clip is **two** actions: take the old one out, then put a new one
in. You can't insert a clip until the old one is out. *(SR3 p.106)*

| Action | Type | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Remove Clip** | Simple | Takes the old clip out of a ready gun. *(p.107)* |
| **Eject Smartgun Clip** | **Free** | Only for a smartgun linked to your smartlink: a cybernetic command drops the clip. *(p.105, p.107)* |
| **Insert Clip** | Simple | Grabs a fresh clip and slams it home. *(p.106)* |

So a full clip change costs:

- **Without a smartlink:** Remove Clip + Insert Clip, **both Simple Actions**.
  That's your whole Combat Phase, and you shoot next phase.
- **With a smartlink and smartgun:** eject as a **Free Action**, insert as a
  Simple Action, and you still have **one Simple Action left to fire**.

The rules don't throw away the rounds left in a removed clip. **Spare clips**
cost **5¥** each, unloaded. Each holds the gun's full capacity, and they
**don't swap between guns**, even guns of the same class. *(SR3 p.281)*

**Refilling empty clips** is the slow part: a **Complex Action per
(Quickness) rounds**. Do it between fights.

## Guns without clips  *(SR3 p.108, p.280)*

Break-action guns, internal magazines and cylinders are reloaded with the
**Reload Firearm** Complex Action. *(SR3 p.108)* Each action loads only part
of the gun:

- **Break action:** 2 rounds per Complex Action.
- **Internal magazine:** (Quickness) rounds per Complex Action.
- **Cylinder:** (Quickness) rounds per Complex Action, or the **whole
  cylinder** in one Complex Action with a **speed loader**.

## Belt-fed weapons  *(SR3 p.280)*

- Inserting a belt is a **Complex Action**.
- Loading rounds into a belt is a Complex Action per **(Quickness × 2)**
  rounds.
- A **100-round belt** costs **4,250¥** and weighs **12.5 kg**. Its
  Concealability is listed as "yeah, right". *(SR3 p.281)*

## Bows and crossbows  *(SR3 p.107, p.118)*

- Nocking an arrow or bolt is the **Ready Weapon** Simple Action. Firing it is
  a separate Fire Weapon action. *(SR3 p.106–107)*
- A character below a crossbow's **Strength Minimum** needs **one extra Ready
  Weapon action per point** short to reload it. *(SR3 p.118)*
- Small throwing weapons, such as knives and shuriken, are readied **½
  Quickness** at a time per Ready Weapon action. *(SR3 p.107)*

## Examples

**Ares Predator, no smartlink.** Liam empties his Predator (15 rounds). Next
Combat Phase he spends a Simple Action on Remove Clip and another on Insert
Clip. His phase is gone, but he's loaded for the next one.

**Ares Predator with a smartlink.** Same situation. Liam ejects with a Free
Action, inserts the new clip with a Simple Action, and fires with his second
Simple Action, all in the same phase.

**Refilling after the fight.** Liam has Quickness 5 and two empty 15-round
clips. Each clip takes 15 ÷ 5 = **3 Complex Actions**, so **6** for both.

**Ruger Super Warhawk.** The six-shot revolver is empty. With Quickness 4
and no speed loader, that's 2 Complex Actions: 4 rounds, then 2. With a
speed loader, it's **one** Complex Action.

**Remington Roomsweeper.** Eight rounds in an internal magazine, Quickness 4:
**2 Complex Actions**.

## Buying and sharing ammunition  *(SR3 p.279, p.281)*

**Ammo is shared by gun class**, using the categories on the Weapon Range
Table *(SR3 p.111)*. Any light pistol can fire any light-pistol ammo, any
assault rifle any assault-rifle ammo, and so on. **All shotguns**, pistol or
rifle, share ammunition. *(SR3 p.279)*

### Ammunition, per 10 rounds  *(SR3 p.281)*

| Type | Availability | Cost | Street Index | Legality |
| :--- | :--- | ---: | :---: | :--- |
| Regular ammo | 2/24 hrs | 20¥ | .75 | As weapon |
| Gel rounds | 4/48 hrs | 30¥ | 1 | As weapon |
| Explosive rounds | 3/36 hrs | 50¥ | .8 | As weapon |
| Taser dart | 6/36 hrs | 50¥ | 1.5 | As weapon |
| Tracer | 3/24 hrs | 75¥ | 1 | As weapon |
| EX explosive | 6/72 hrs | 100¥ | 1.5 | As weapon |
| Flechette rounds | 3/36 hrs | 100¥ | .8 | As weapon |
| APDS | 14/14 days | 70¥ | 4 | 3-M |
| Assault cannon | 5/3 days | 450¥ | 2 | As weapon |

Ammunition is Concealability 8 (assault-cannon rounds and taser darts 3), and
**−1 Concealability for every extra 10 rounds**. For belted ammo, add
(rounds ÷ 100) to the Availability. *(SR3 p.281)*

For what each round type does in combat (APDS, explosive, flechette, gel,
tracer), see **Ammunition** *(SR3 p.116)*. For how to buy on the street, see
[Buying Gear & Fencing](../../street/gear-and-fencing/).
