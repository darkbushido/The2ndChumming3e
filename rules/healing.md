# Healing
{: .no_toc }

How Stun and Physical damage recover, first aid, magical healing, doctors
and hospital bills. All rules are from the core rulebook, *(SR3 p.125–129)*
and *(SR3 p.193–194)*.

1. TOC
{:toc}

{: .fixed }
> Original gist: "Healing Rules Reference".
> - **Stun**: TN is **2 plus your injury modifiers**, not 2 plus the number of
>   boxes filled. Recovering **one box** takes **60 minutes ÷ successes**. You
>   roll the **higher** of Body or Willpower; that's the rule, not a GM option.
> - **First aid**: TN is **4 / 6 / 8 / 10** by wound level. One success lowers
>   the wound **one Damage Level**, never more. It doesn't heal a box per
>   success, and there is no "capped by medkit rating".
> - **Heal / Treat**: TN is **10 − the patient's Essence**, not the number of
>   boxes filled. Each success heals **one box, up to the spell's Force**.
>   Heal has no +2 penalty after an hour.
> - **Natural healing** uses the Healing Table (TN 4 / 6 / 8 / 10), and the
>   base time is divided by your successes. The "nurse / hospital / DocWagon"
>   modifiers in the original were invented. The real ones are on the
>   Doctoring Table below.
> - The fourth level is **Deadly**, not "Lethal".

## Condition Monitor recap  *(SR3 p.125–126)*

Stun and Physical each have **10 boxes**. The **Light / Moderate / Serious**
thresholds are 1 / 3 / 6 boxes, and they add **+1 / +2 / +3** to target
numbers and **−1 / −2 / −3** to Initiative. At 10 boxes you are unconscious
(Deadly). Stun that overflows 10 goes into the Physical track. Physical
overflow can reach your **Body** rating before you die, and you lose another
box every (Body) Combat Turns until stabilized. See [Actions &
Combat](../combat/#wounds-and-the-condition-monitor) for the full table.

## Stun damage  *(SR3 p.126)*

- Roll **Body or Willpower, whichever is higher**, against **TN 2**, plus
  your current Stun and Physical injury modifiers.
- Recovering **1 box** takes **60 minutes ÷ successes**. When the time is up,
  erase the box, then test again for the next one.
- You must be **resting completely**. If you're interrupted, the process
  stops and you roll again, and the new result can't be better than the
  first.
- A character knocked out by Deadly Stun damage doesn't wake until the Stun
  is back down to **Serious**.
- No medicine and no known spell heals Stun. Stim patches only mask it, at a
  cost. *(SR3 p.126, p.305)*

## Physical damage  *(SR3 p.126–127)*

### Do you need a doctor?

Make a **Body Test**, natural Body only with no cyberware, against the TN
for your overall wound level:

| Wound level | TN |
| :--- | :---: |
| Light | 2 |
| Moderate | 4 |
| Serious | 6 |
| Deadly | always needs medical attention |

With any successes, you'll heal without medical attention. With none, you
need medical attention to heal at all. Apply first aid and magical healing
**before** making this test. *(SR3 p.126–127)*

### Stages of healing  *(SR3 p.127)*

Physical damage heals **one Damage Level at a time**: Deadly to Serious,
Serious to Moderate, and so on. When a level heals, the track drops to the
**lowest box** of the next level down. A Serious wound that heals to
Moderate leaves exactly 3 boxes filled.

For each stage, make a **Body Test** against the TN below. Divide the **base
time** by your successes. The result can never be less than the **minimum
time**.

### Healing Table  *(SR3 p.127)*

| Damage level | Base time | Minimum time | TN | Minimum lifestyle |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Deadly | 30 days | 3 days | 10 | Hospitalized |
| Serious | 20 days | 2 days | 8 | High |
| Moderate | 10 days | 1 day | 6 | Middle |
| Light | 24 hours | 2 hours | 4 | Low |

If you can't support the minimum lifestyle while you heal, the GM may add
modifiers. Lifestyle can be paid by the day: the monthly cost ÷ 30.
*(SR3 p.127)*

### Doctoring Table  *(SR3 p.128)*

| Situation | Modifier |
| :--- | :---: |

| Long-term magical care | −2 |

| Not in a hospital or clinic | +2 |
| Bad conditions | +3 |

| Service | Deadly | Serious | Moderate | Light |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |

## First aid (Biotech)  *(SR3 p.129)*

- It must be applied **within one hour** of the injury, and it **can't help
  once magical healing has been applied**.
- Make a **Biotech Test** against the TN for the current wound. **At least
  one success lowers the Damage Level by one.** First aid can never lower it
  by more than one level.
- Treatment time ÷ successes = the number of **uninterrupted Combat Turns**
  it takes. A serious interruption means you start again.
- First aid only helps **Physical** damage.

### First Aid Table  *(SR3 p.129)*

| Damage level | TN | Treatment time |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Light | 4 | 5 Combat Turns |
| Moderate | 6 | 10 Combat Turns |
| Serious | 8 | 15 Combat Turns |
| Deadly | 10 | Special (stabilize only) |

| Situation | Modifier |
| :--- | :---: |
| Patient is Awakened | +2 |
| Bad conditions (a city street) | +1 |
| Terrible conditions (the same street in a firefight in the rain) | +3 |
| Patient's Body 1–3 / 4–6 / 7–9 / 10+ | +0 / −1 / −2 / −3 |
| No medkit available | +4 |

### Deadly wounds  *(SR3 p.129)*

- First aid can **stabilize** a Deadly wound but can't heal it. **One
  success** stops the extra box of damage every (Body) Combat Turns.
- If the Biotech Test fails, the patient rolls **natural Body against TN
  10**. With a success they stabilize themselves. If that fails too, they die
  once the damage exceeds their Body rating.
- **Trauma patch**: gives the patient an extra Body Test to stabilize,
  against TN **4 + the rating of any dermal armor or blood filters**. It also
  raises the chance of permanent damage.

## Magical healing  *(SR3 p.127, p.193–194)*

| Spell | Type | TN | Duration | Drain |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Heal** | Mana | 10 − Essence | Permanent | (Wound Level) |
| **Treat** | Mana | 10 − Essence | Permanent | −1(Wound Level) |

- **Each success heals one box** of Physical damage, **up to the spell's
  Force**. Successes can also be spent dividing the base time before the
  spell becomes permanent. You can split them between the two as you like.
  *(SR3 p.194)*
- **Treat** must be cast **within one hour** of the injury, and becomes
  permanent in **half** the normal time. **Heal** can be cast at any time.
  *(SR3 p.194)*
- The Drain **Damage Level** is the patient's wound level when the spell is
  cast. *(SR3 p.194)*
- **Once only**: a character can be magically healed only once for a single
  set of injuries. A successful Heal or Treat rules out any further healing
  spells **and first aid**, so do the first aid first. *(SR3 p.127, p.194)*
- **Physical damage from Drain can't be healed by magic**, only by rest and
  medical care. *(SR3 p.162)*
- All health spells need the caster to **touch** the patient. *(SR3 p.193)*

## Awakened patients and permanent damage

**Magic loss** *(SR3 p.129)*: when an Awakened character takes a Deadly
wound, or is treated without the +2 Awakened modifier, roll **2D6**. On a
result **at or below** their current Magic, they permanently lose 1 Magic.
For a Deadly wound treated without the +2, roll twice.

**Deadly wounds and permanent damage** *(SR3 p.127–128)*: after a Deadly
wound, roll **Body against TN 4** (+2 if a trauma patch was used; dermal
armor counts).

- **0 successes**: a vital organ or system is gravely damaged. The whole
  healing time doubles, and 1D6 sets which attribute is permanently lost:
  Body, Strength, Quickness, Intelligence, Willpower or Reaction.
- **1 success**: an eye or limb is lost and must be replaced. Base healing
  time goes up by 50 percent.
- **2 or more**: no lasting damage.
