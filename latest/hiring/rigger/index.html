  • The Defiance T-250 is a shotgun (SR3 p.278), not a rotorcraft. “Ares Duelist” and “Fly-Spy” drones don’t appear in any book checked, so they’ve been removed.
  • A jumped-in rigger doesn’t feel every hit. Damage carries over only when the vehicle takes Serious damage (6M) or is destroyed (6S). It’s resisted with Willpower. (SR3 p.145)
  • The Scenario C total was 30,000¥, not 25,000–30,000¥.

What the rigger is risking

  • Their body. A jumped-in rigger resists 6M Physical when the vehicle takes Serious damage and 6S when it’s destroyed, using Willpower with no pool dice. (SR3 p.145)
  • Their implants. A Vehicle Control Rig costs (SR3 p.302):
VCR Essence Cost
Level 1 2 12,000¥
Level 2 3 60,000¥
Level 3 5 300,000¥
  • Their vehicles. Whatever is shot down comes out of the rigger’s pocket unless the contract says otherwise.

House rates

These rates aren’t from any SR3 book. They are the original gist’s numbers, kept for convenience.

Base day rate, by vehicle or gunnery skill and VCR

Tier Skill and VCR Base day rate Typical hardware
Amateur Skill 1–3, no VCR or VCR 1 300–600¥ An ordinary ground vehicle; no combat drones
Professional Skill 4–5, VCR 1–2 1,500–3,000¥ An upgraded getaway car and one or two surveillance drones
Veteran Skill 6–7, VCR 2–3 4,000–8,000¥ An armored vehicle, a remote control deck, and combat drones such as a Doberman or Steel Lynx
Elite Skill 8+, VCR 3 12,000¥ and up Security or military aircraft, heavy weapons, a drone swarm

Threat multiplier

Work Multiplier
Transport or recon, avoiding trouble ×1
Combat support or a getaway under fire ×2
Heavy combat, anti-vehicle fire or assault ×4–6

Hazard pay and expenses

  • Remote (deck or autonav): no hazard pay.
  • Jumped in with a VCR: +50%, because the rigger feels Serious and Destroyed damage.
  • The employer pays for ammunition, fuel and expended countermeasures.
  • For high-threat work, a deposit of 25–50% of each drone’s replacement value.
