Hiring a Combat Medic

A runner with Biotech and a medkit, who comes along to keep the team alive until it reaches a real doctor. No SR3 book has a “combat medic” archetype or a price for hiring one. This page puts together what the books do price, and adds house rates in the same style as the other specialist pages.

What the books give you

    Source
Paramedic first aid, per wound treated Light 50¥, Moderate 100¥, Serious 200¥, Deadly 400¥ SR3 p.128
Doctor’s services, per day the same scale: 50¥–400¥ a day by wound SR3 p.128
Bodyguard/security duty, the nearest run type 200¥/day SRComp p.100
DocWagon contract, the alternative to hiring a medic Basic 5,000¥, Gold 25,000¥, Platinum 50,000¥, Super-Platinum 100,000¥ a year SR3 p.304

The book’s closest character is the DocWagon paramedic contact: Biotech 5 with a First Aid specialisation at 7, Boosted Reflexes 1, an armor jacket and a Rating 5 medkit. They get their thrills on the job, they’re curious about runners, and if you stay on their good side they’ll patch you up cheaply (MJLBB p.65). That’s a fair picture of a professional-tier medic below.

What a medic actually does at the table

  • First aid reduces a Light, Moderate or Serious wound by one level, and must be given within an hour of the injury. On a Deadly wound it can only stabilize the patient. See the full procedure on Healing (SR3 p.129).
  • Know when your patient is Awakened. First aid on an Awakened patient carries a +2 target number modifier. A magician treated without that modifier, or given a Deadly wound, risks permanently losing Magic (SR3 p.129). A medic who takes the +2 and avoids the high-tech medicines is worth the extra time to a mage.
  • Biotech can’t help once magical healing has been applied (SR3 p.129). The medic and the team’s healer need to agree on who goes first.

The medic’s kit (SR3 p.304–305)

Item Cost Notes

House rates

These rates aren’t from any SR3 book. They follow the house-rule framework that the other specialist pages use, and are scaled to match them.

Base day rate, by Biotech

Tier Biotech Base day rate Typical kit
Corpsman 1–3 200–400¥ A medkit and a couple of trauma patches
Paramedic 4–5 800–2,000¥ A good medkit, slap patches, maybe ex-DocWagon
Trauma specialist 6–7 2,500–5,000¥ A stabilization unit, combat experience
Elite 8+ 8,000¥ and up A deluxe stabilization unit, and a surgeon’s hands under fire

Threat multiplier

Work Multiplier
On call or waiting at the safehouse ×1
With the team on a run, light opposition expected ×2
Pulling casualties out under fire (a High Threat Response, in DocWagon terms) ×3–4

Hazard pay and expenses

  • Off-site, at the safehouse or the van: no hazard pay.
  • On-site with the team: +100%.
  • Consumables at book price: every trauma patch and medkit restock used is billed on top.
  • Or bill per patient, at the SR3 p.128 paramedic scale. A medic hired for the night and kept off-site may prefer that.
