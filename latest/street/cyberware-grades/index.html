Cyberware Grades & Used Chrome

Basic, alpha, beta and deltaware; buying used; removing chrome; and selling what you’ve pulled out. Grade rules are from Man & Machine: Cyberware.

  2. Used cyberware (M&M p.11, p.45)

Original gist: “Cyberware Grades & Secondhand Gear Guide”.

  • Betaware is ×.6 Essence, not ×.7.
  • Used cyberware costs the Essence of its grade, not ×1.25. It costs half price, not ×.75.
  • The availability modifiers were invented. Alphaware is as available as basic. Betaware is +5 to the TN and ×1.5 the time. Deltaware is at least +9 and ×3. Used is standard.
  • Betaware isn’t available to starting characters either, not only deltaware.
  • Used deltaware can’t be bought, but if you get hold of some, it can be installed as betaware. It isn’t “destroyed on extraction”.
  • Used implants come with 1D3 permanent Stress Points, not “1 to 2”.
  • Removing chrome never gives Essence back. A later implant can use the “Essence hole”, but only through a surgical option at +2 Threshold.
  • The 1–3 hour harvest window, the TN 4–8 extraction roll and the “Electronics (Cyberware) scrub” aren’t in Man & Machine.

The four grades (M&M p.10–11)

  • Basic is mass-market and fits anyone. Any street doc can install it.
  • Alphaware has better materials and testing. It’s as easy to get as basic and costs twice as much.
  • Betaware is custom-fitted to your body, after tests and DNA screening, and uses myomers in place of gyros and microengines. It must be special-ordered.
  • Deltaware is built during the surgery itself, in corporate clinics that are almost never mentioned. It isn’t available on the street.

Cyberware Grades Table (M&M p.45)

Grade Essence Cost multiplier Availability
Basic ×1 ×1 Standard
Alpha ×.8 (−20%) ×2 Standard
Beta ×.6 (−40%) ×4 +5 / ×1.5 time
Delta ×.5 (−50%) ×8 +9 / ×3 time, at least
Used by grade ×.5 Standard
  • Round Essence costs up. A reduction can’t take an Essence cost below .01. (M&M p.45)
  • The cost multiplier covers the implant only. Surgery and hospital bills are extra. (M&M p.45; see Surgery, M&M p.135)
  • Most shadow clinics can supply basic, alpha and often beta. Betaware isn’t available to starting characters. A clinic that does delta has to want you there. (M&M p.45)

Matching grades (M&M p.45)

Accessories for an alpha, beta or delta device must be the same grade. So must every component of a system bought in parts, such as a smartlink. Devices installed inside a cyberlimb or body compartment don’t have to match the limb.

Used cyberware (M&M p.11, p.45)

  • Basic, alpha and beta chrome can be bought used. It’s cheap, it’s of suspect quality, and it still works.
  • Used deltaware can’t be bought. If you acquire some anyway, it’s installed as if it were betaware.
  • Used chrome is as easy to find as basic, but the sellers are organleggers, ghouls and dubious clinics.
  • Its Essence cost is the same as new of that grade. Its price is half.
  • Each used item comes with 1D3 permanent Stress Points. They can never be removed, and they add to any Stress it picks up in use. (M&M p.45; see Damage and Healing, M&M p.124)

Example: Wired Reflexes, level 1

Wired Reflexes 1 is 2 Essence, 55,000¥, Availability 4/8 days. (SR3 p.302)

Grade Essence Cost Availability
Basic 2.0 55,000¥ 4 / 8 days
Alpha 1.6 110,000¥ 4 / 8 days
Beta 1.2 220,000¥ 9 / 12 days
Delta 1.0 440,000¥ 13 / 24 days or worse
Used basic 2.0 27,500¥ 4 / 8 days, plus 1D3 Stress
Used alpha 1.6 55,000¥ 4 / 8 days, plus 1D3 Stress

“Used” is shown here as half the price of the same grade new. The table’s ×.5 sits in the Cost column beside “by grade” for Essence, which reads as a discount applied to whichever grade it is.

Removing cyberware (M&M p.147, p.150)

  • Removal doesn’t give back lost Essence. (M&M p.147)
  • The implant takes 1D6 ÷ 2 Stress coming out. If the procedure fails, it stays in, still takes the Stress, and has to make a Stress Test. (M&M p.147)
  • Essence Slot is a surgical option at +2 Threshold. A new implant can be fitted into the “Essence hole” left by removed chrome, and the old implant’s Essence cost is subtracted from the new one’s. Without this option there’s no discount. (M&M p.150)

Removal is surgery, and uses the procedures from Surgery onward. (M&M p.135 on)

Selling harvested chrome

Man & Machine has no separate resale rule for used implants, so sell them the same way as any other loot. Use the fencing rules (SR3 p.237–238): a 30% base, 5% per net success on the Negotiation, never below 10% or above 50%. Stolen chrome isn’t standard gear, so expect the Finding a Fence TN to rise.

Example. A dead samurai’s smartlink (2,500¥) and retractable spur (11,500¥) (SR3 p.302) list at 14,000¥ together. The fence’s base offer is 4,200¥. Depending on the Negotiation, the deal can’t go below 1,400¥ or above 7,000¥.

Optional flavor, not from the books. Tracking tags or corporate lockouts on stolen high-end implants, and a time limit for pulling chrome from a corpse, are good complications. If you use them, set the tests yourself; neither book gives numbers.

Being scanned (SR3 p.237)

Cyberware scanners are usually rated 3–9. The scanner rolls its rating against TN 3 for typical cyberware and TN 6 for alphaware. More successes identify the implants more precisely.
