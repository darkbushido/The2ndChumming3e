Reading a weapon’s statistics (SR3 p.275–276)

Statistic What it means
Concealability The higher, the easier to hide.
Reach (melee) The weapon’s length. A longer reach gives a target number modifier in melee (SR3 p.121).
Damage Power and Damage Level, like 9M. A melee weapon’s Power is usually the wielder’s Strength plus a number: (STR + 2)M. Stun marks Stun damage, (f) that flechette is already counted.
Ammo Rounds held and how it reloads: (c) clip, (b) break-action, (m) internal magazine, (cy) cylinder, (belt) belt feed. See Reloading.
Mode SS single-shot, SA semi-automatic, BF burst-fire, FA full-auto. Below.
RC Recoil compensation built in. A number in brackets only counts with every integral accessory in use, such as a folding stock.
Legal The Legality Code, like 6P-E (SR3 p.274).

Firearms take standard or caseless ammunition, but not both. Every firearm has a digital ammo counter (SR3 p.276).

  • Bows and crossbows use their Strength Minimum, not the archer’s Strength, for range: a Strength Minimum 5 bow has a short range of 0–5 meters and a medium range of 6–50 (SR3 p.110). Thrown weapons use the thrower’s Strength (SR3 p.118).
  • Magnifying scopes move the range one column left per point of rating, but never past Short (SR3 p.110).
  • Guns of one class share ammunition: every light pistol can use any light pistol ammo, and every shotgun any shotgun ammo (SR3 p.279).

Firing modes (SR3 p.114–116)

Mode Action Effect
Single-shot (SS) Simple Can’t fire again in the same Combat Phase.
Semi-automatic (SA) Simple Two shots a phase. The second takes +1 recoil.
Burst-fire (BF) Simple Three rounds. Power +3, Damage Level +1. +3 recoil per burst; two bursts a phase.
Full-auto (FA) Complex Declare 3 to 10 rounds. Power +1 per round; Damage Level +1 per full three rounds, up to Deadly. +1 recoil per round.
  • Recoil compensation cancels recoil, and only recoil. Every shot or burst is a separate test with its own Combat Pool dice (SR3 p.115).
  • Short bursts. A burst of 2 rounds, because the clip ran out, gets +2 Power, no Damage Level increase, and +2 recoil. A burst of 1 round is a single shot (SR3 p.115).
  • Heavy weapons double uncompensated recoil: a medium machine gun firing 10 rounds with 6 points of compensation has (10 − 6) × 2 = +8. So does a shotgun in burst-fire (SR3 p.111).
  • Several targets: +2 to the TN for each new target engaged that Combat Phase. Full-auto fire walked between targets wastes 1 round per meter between them; smartguns waste none (SR3 p.116).

The book’s example (SR3 p.115–116): Wedge fires an AK-97 (8M) with a gas vent 3 and a shock pad, 4 points of compensation, and a laser sight (−1). Base TN 4 − 1 = 3.

  • Burst one, 3 rounds at the first punk: 3 recoil, all compensated. TN 3, damage 8 + 3 = 11S.
  • Burst two, 3 rounds at the second: 6 rounds fired, 2 uncompensated, +2 for the second target. TN 3 + 2 + 2 = 7, 11S.
  • Burst three, 4 rounds at the third: 10 rounds, 6 uncompensated, +4 for the third target. TN 3 + 6 + 4 = 13, damage 8 + 4 = 12S.

Ammunition (SR3 p.116–117, p.279–281)

One type per clip, except tracers. Damage codes assume regular ammo.

Type Effect
APDS Halves (round down) armor’s Ballistic rating and Barrier Ratings. Normal ammo against vehicles and drones.
Explosive +1 Power. Through a barrier: TN is twice the Barrier Rating, and the barrier takes damage as if at half. Misfires when every die rolls a 1: the shooter is hit by the weapon’s normal damage, resisted without Combat Pool.
EX Explosive +2 Power, otherwise as explosive.
Flechette +1 Damage Level against unarmored targets. Against armor, use double Impact or Ballistic, whichever is higher. Double Barrier Ratings and vehicle armor. Dermal armor cancels the level increase.
Gel −2 Power, same level, all Stun. Impact armor applies, not Ballistic. Knockdown: Body against the attack’s Power.
Tracer Full-auto only, every third round. Without a smartgun, −1 TN beyond Short per three rounds fired. The tracers add no Power to the burst, but do raise its level: a 5M SMG firing 10 rounds does 12D, not 15D.

Prices per 10 rounds are on Reloading. Standard ammunition is 20¥ for 10 rounds (SR3 p.279).

Shotguns (SR3 p.117)

The listed shotguns fire slugs. Shot rounds use the flechette rules on the gun’s Damage Code, and spread:

  • Set the choke from 2 to 10. Every choke meters the shot travels, the pattern widens by a meter.
  • Each widening costs 1 Power and gives the attacker −1 TN. At Power 0 the shot is spent.
  • Everyone in the spread is a target. The attacker rolls once; each target dodges and resists against those successes, with +1 Damage Resistance die per other target in front of them.
  • A smartlink is worth −1 with shot. Smart goggles and laser sights don’t help.

Doing it in The 2nd Chumming (Foundry)

Getting a weapon

Drag a weapon onto the character from the compendiums, or buy it with 🛒 Buy gear. It appears on the Weapons tab. Its category picks the skill and the range bands; Range override on the weapon replaces the bands, as “5/15/30/50”.

Shooting

  • ✋ marks a weapon ready in hand; drawing one is a Ready Weapon action (SR3 p.107).
  • The 🎲 on the row opens the attack. Choose the fire mode and, for full-auto, the rounds. The dialog shows the range band measured on the map, the recoil after compensation (the weapon’s own and the character’s, both editable), the target count for this phase, and every other modifier. The TN is yours to change before you roll.
  • Smartgun, laser sight and recoil compensation are fields on the weapon. A smartgun saves the rounds walked fire would waste.
  • A shotgun loaded with shot asks for the choke, and the card shows each spread’s Power and TN.
  • 🎯 on the token’s HUD fires a ready weapon from the map.

Ammunition

  • ↻ Reload loads from ammunition on the character: loose rounds of the gun’s class, or a spare clip. The loaded type (regular, APDS, explosive, gel, flechette, tracer, shot) changes the damage and the armor used on the soak card by itself.
  • With the world setting Track ammunition on, firing counts rounds down, and an empty gun won’t fire until reloaded.
  • Thrown weapons count down their quantity; bows and crossbows nock an arrow or bolt.

Melee

Equip a melee weapon on the Weapons tab, then use its 🎲 Melee attack. Reach is read from the weapon. Unarmed is always there, and uses the best of Unarmed Combat and any martial art. See Actions & Combat.
