# SR3 Table Reference

Quick-reference pages for **Shadowrun, Third Edition**: combat, healing,
grenades, magic, life with and without a SIN, buying and fencing gear,
cyberware grades, and what it costs to hire a specialist.

These pages began as fifteen Gemini-generated gists. Every rule has since
been checked against the rulebooks, and each one now carries a citation
like *(SR3 p.113)* so you can check it at the table. Abbreviations are on
[Source books & method](sources/).

## How to read the markers

**Show corrections**, the checkbox at the top of every page, turns on
yellow boxes listing what was **wrong in the original gist** and has been
corrected. The originals got a lot wrong: invented tables, rules from other
editions, and numbers that appear in no book. The boxes are hidden by default,
and the setting is remembered in your browser.

{: .fixed }
> This is what a correction box looks like.

{: .house }
> A purple box marks material that is **not in any SR3 book**. The hiring
> rates are the main example. It's kept because it's useful at the table,
> but treat it as a house rule and change it freely.

Anything without a citation or a purple box is general description, not a
rule.
