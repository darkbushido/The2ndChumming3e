# Release notes

What changed, for the people running games with it. Newest first.

## 0.6.0 — 2026-09-23

This is the **first tagged release**. There was no prior tag, so everything below — 522 commits —
is the project's whole history, not a delta since a previous version. Most of it is invisible at
the table (tooling, packs, tests). What follows is a hand-written summary of what actually
changes how a rule resolves, with its book and printed page, aimed at the GM rather than the
diff. The bucketed commit log after it is the full history for reference, not something to read
end to end.

### What matters at the table

**Dice and pools**
- **Rule of One** fires only when *every* die comes up 1, and its consequence is GM judgement,
  not a mechanical penalty (SR3 p.38) — there is no second "glitch" tier.
- **Defaulting** (SR3 p.84-85) rolls your **full** rating in the default skill; only the *pool*
  dice are capped at half. Reading the table as "the dice to roll" (a common mistake) halves
  every defaulted test.
- **Combat, Spell, Astral and Hacking Pools all refresh once per Combat Turn** (once per Foundry
  round), never per pass and never per combat (SR3 p.104).

**Ranged and melee combat**
- **Dodging**: a *tie* between attacker and defender successes is a **hit**, not a dodge (SR3
  p.113). A failed dodge isn't wasted — its successes carry into the soak. The defender declares
  **after** seeing the attack's successes, which is RAW, not a house convenience.
- **Damage staging past Deadly**: ranged and Matrix discard surplus successes at Deadly; **melee
  and astral combat let surplus successes raise Power instead** (SR3 p.113 vs p.122).
- **Melee ties go to the attacker** (SR3 p.122) — the opposite of the ranged dodge tie.
- **Reach** is a differential the longer-reach fighter can apply to either side's TN, not a flat
  subtraction each side takes on their own (SR3 p.121).
- **Grenades now stage by net successes**, the same as any other attack (SR3 p.119) — previously
  the thrower's roll only tightened scatter and never affected how hard the blast hit.

**Magic**
- **Elemental Manipulation spells** (Fireball, Lightning Bolt, Acid Stream, Ball Lightning, …) are
  now correctly resolved as ranged attacks — dodgeable, soaked with Body + Combat Pool, Impact
  armor at half (SR3 p.183, p.196) — instead of going through the plain Spell Resistance path
  meant for Combat spells (Manabolt, Powerbolt, Stunbolt), which was blocking their dodge, their
  armor, and even their Damage Level choice.
- **A glitched Sorcery Test (all 1s) now correctly adds +2 to the caster's own Drain TN** (SR3
  p.182), on top of and separate from sustaining penalties.
- **Spells now record the Force they were learned at**, and the cast dialog won't let you cast
  higher (SR3 p.178).
- **Mystic Armor now protects in astral combat**, as the book states outright (SR3 p.170, p.175)
  — it previously applied everywhere except the one place named by name.
- **Sustained spells** add +2 to every test you make, including Drain, while held (SR3 p.178).
- 117 adept powers were audited end to end; **Attribute Boost, Improved Ability, Improved
  Reflexes, Missile Parry and the direct-effect powers (Combat Sense, Pain Resistance, Mystic
  Armor, Killing Hands, Penetrating Strike)** are implemented against the book, most for the
  first time.

**Essence and cyberware**
- **Essence loss is permanent** — removing cyberware does not refund it (M&M p.147). A GM can
  still correct a player's error by hand.
- **Cyberware grades** (Alpha/Beta/Delta/Used) apply their Essence multiplier (M&M p.45).
- **Cyberzombies, Chronic Dissociation Syndrome, cybersystem Stress and the Wound Effect Table**
  are all built out (M&M pp.50-59, 124-131).

**Gear, ammo, healing, drugs, karma**
- **Ammunition is tracked** per the Ammo Reloading Table (SR3 p.280) — clips, cylinders, belts,
  internal magazines and break-actions each cost what the book says, and it's on by default for
  new worlds.
- **Karma spending** follows the Skill Improvement Cost Table exactly, including several
  under-the-radar arithmetic errors fixed along the way (rounding, specialisation caps, the
  Racial Modified Limit multiplier) — see TODO 80 in the archive for the full list.
- **Guided healing** and **drug** (addiction/tolerance/withdrawal) flows now exist, matching the
  Healing Table and M&M's drug rules respectively.
- **Buying gear** models the Availability/Street Index/negotiation flow from SR3 pp.272-273.
